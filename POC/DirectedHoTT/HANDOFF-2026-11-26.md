## The new situation: master has a graded judgment

Plans 0.146 and 0.157 (branch `origin/0.146-linear-core-4`, 18 commits on master `8aa064525`; merged
together later) compile every Once program through a LINEAR core. Part A, the spike, is closed. B,
the integration, runs on three architectures, with `NoLeak` and a bridge `Lq` from the elaborator's
graded `Expr`. Cost against master is now 1.62× run time and 2.18× `.text`; plan 0.157 S2 is next.
D468 (proposed) retires the IR: L1 passes run over the Spec's graded `Tm`, and "the dependent kernel
extends that judgment (D231)".

Master's core judgment (`formal/Once/Spec/Core/Typing.agda`):

    Δ ⊩ Γ ⊢[ Ψ ] t ∷ A ! π

- usage `Ψ` over `0 ≤ 1 ≤ ω` (QTT, affine), with `⊢sub-use`;
- effect `π` over `pure ≤ act ≤ eff`, with `⊢sub-eff` (the monad morphism along the order);
- the coupling law: an erased position (0) is pure (`positionGrade`);
- arrows carry both (`A ⇒[ mk-kind q π ] B`), and a `ref` is pure with usage zero;
- ν, `unfold` and `iter` (servers, at `eff`) are core formers.

This POC's kernel judgment is `Γ ⊢ t ∷ A`. It has **no usage, no effect grade, no subsumption and no
codata**. The OCP0009 QTT spikes (`NbEPQTT*`, `NbEPLinQTT`) graded only simple types. ROADMAP R5
named only {0,1,ω}, as a layer to add later. That framing was wrong: the grades are the shape of the
judgment that the dependent kernel has to extend.

## Next steps, in order

1. **Write PLAN-GRADED (ROADMAP R5, rewritten today).** It designs the graded dependent judgment
   against master's `Spec.Grade` and `Spec.Core.Typing`, argued from the mathematics, not copied: agreement with the
   compiler is evidence, not the justification. The questions, for the user:
   - **Usage in types.** Types and indices are checked at 0 (Atkey's QTT); Π carries the
     argument's usage. How grades meet `Hom`/`Id`/`tr`/`ap`, IMu, `Desc` and the indices at 0.
   - **Effects in a dependent kernel.** Can a type mention only pure terms? Does conversion
     evaluate only pure terms, so that NbE never runs an effect? Master's `positionGrade` already
     gives this half: 0 ⇒ pure.
   - **Codata.** Plan 0.147 §5 says "no codata in the kernel", but master's core has ν/`unfold`/
     `iter`. Either the dependent kernel gets ν at the effect grades, or dependency covers only the
     pure fragment, with ν non-dependent beside it.
   - **Subsumption** (`⊢sub-use`, `⊢sub-eff`) under conversion and substitution.
   - **Signature references.** Master's `ref` is typed from a schema with no δ; ours has δ by
     erasure (S5 route B) and projections (D501). One of them has to move.
2. **Carry the graded judgment through `Spec/` and `Metatheory/`** (SR, confluence, canonicity,
   consistency), then through NbE and CheckA. Kernel changes are cheap; the Knot is where cost lands.
3. **One Knot family as a pilot, on the graded judgment**, built with the generic `⊢inst`. Measure
   what grades cost in the Knot before migrating any further.
4. **The remaining families** (Red, RedT, Judge, Conv, Lookup, Sub, …), generated lines toward zero,
   and every Knot module under 10 s. Then the object-level `prog` exhibit.
5. **Answer what master asks** (plan 0.147 §5, `INPUT-DATA-AND-STRUCTURES.md`): opaque or nominal
   entries (δ scoped by segment), K-free matching, records and Σ for theories, laws as `Hom`.
