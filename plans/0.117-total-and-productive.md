# Plan 0.117 — Once is total and productive, and a server is an ordinary function

## 0. Why

GOAL.md requires servers. Today no Once program can run forever: `Eff A B` means `A → T B` and
`T` is inductive, so every effectful function ends after finitely many events (D311 §Context).
And the Spec does not state the property the language is built on — that it is TOTAL (a pure
computation is a value) and PRODUCTIVE (each next event comes after finite work). D311 decides
both: a T+P part in `Spec.Metatheory`, the free completely iterative monad for effectful
computation that may run forever, guarded iteration as the server construct, `main` unchanged.

## 1. The mathematics (what a reader of the Spec should find)

* `T X` — the free monad on the call/halt signature `Σ`: the INITIAL algebra of
  `Y ↦ X + Σ Y`. Finite trees. Effectful computation that returns.
* `T∞ X` — the free completely iterative monad: the FINAL coalgebra of the same functor. Possibly
  infinite trees, no silent step. `T ↪ T∞`.
* Guarded iteration: for `f : A → T (B + A)` guarded (an event before every `inr`),
  `iter f : A → T∞ B` is the unique `x` with `x = f >=> [ret, x]` (up to bisimilarity).
* T+P: `M pure X = X`; `T X` is well-founded (`FiniteT`); every `T∞` tree is productive by the
  shape of its functor (no silent node); `Behavior n` of any program is computed by a total
  function of `n`.

### C. The grade algebra and its domains
Staged so each step is a green tree:
* C0 PER-PREMISE GRADES over today's `{pure, eff}` — DONE in the core: `_·p_` (sequencing; equal
  to the join on a line) in `Spec.Type.Grade`; the core rules that sequence premises (`app`,
  `let`, `pair`, `case`'s scrutinee, `fold`, `unfold`, `out`) take a grade per premise and
  conclude at the product; the graded bind `bindᵍ π₁ π₂ : M π₁ X → (X → M π₂ Y) → M (π₁ ·p π₂) Y`
  and its law `toT-bindᵍ` (`Effects`); `⊢composeᶜ`/`⊢pairᶜ` take `π₂ ·p π₁ ⊑π π` (their arrow
  is at any grade above the product). The SURFACE still shares one grade in `compose`/`pair`
  and reads it as the diagonal (`·p-diag`); it moves to per-premise grades with `eff⁺` (C2),
  where the checker has to synthesize least grades anyway.
* C1 `prod` and `T∞` — DONE (D311 §Landed):
  - domain (`Spec.Iterative`, laws in `Denotation.TraceMonadInfLaws`): `T∞` a coinductive record over the
    three nodes of `T`; `_>>=∞_`; `embed : T → T∞` (a monad morphism up to `≈∞`); bisimilarity
    `TreeEq∞` with `≈∞-bind`/`-sym`/`-rewrite`/`-map`; `events∞ ι h m n`, total in `n`, and
    `events∞-embed` (a finite tree observes as its run); `ν∞` (a stream whose layers are `T∞`).
  - the grade: `Purity` gains `prod`; `⊑π` its line; `M prod = T∞`; `⟦ ν-type F prod ⟧ᵛ = ν∞`.
  - ONE erasure, `toT∞ : M π X → T∞ X`, replaces `toT` (which cannot exist at `prod`):
    coherence's relation and `Spec.Equivalence`'s observation are stated over it (`ObsM` the
    first `n` layers of the tree — still "no finite observation"); the monad laws per grade up
    to `EqM π` (`≡` at `pure`/`eff`, `≈∞` at `prod`); `Effects`' erasure laws up to `≈∞`.
  - the compiler side: `GradedRelation` relates a `prod` meaning to the IR's (finite) tree
    through `embed`; `HonestFFI` keeps `prod` out of FFI types (a foreign call is one event).
* C2 `eff⁺` and `T⁺` — the guard as a grade (G2) — DONE in the Spec, coherence and compiler
  (D311 §Landed C2), except the surface's per-premise grades (C2b):
  - `T⁺ X`: a tree whose root is an EVENT (`call⁺ o a k` with `k` into `T X`, or `halt⁺`) — the
    ideal of the free monad; `T` acts on it on the right (`T⁺ X → (X → T Y) → T⁺ Y`) and it
    embeds into `T` (and so `T∞`). `M eff⁺ = T⁺`.
  - order: `pure ⊑ eff`, `eff⁺ ⊑ eff ⊑ prod`; `pure` and `eff⁺` incomparable (join `eff`).
    Product: `pure` the unit, `prod` absorbing, `eff⁺` absorbing among the finite grades
    (`eff ·p eff⁺ = eff⁺ ·p eff = eff⁺`).
  - `eff⁺` has NO unit: `M` is a graded monad over the ordered monoid, with `returnM` only at
    grades above `pure`. So the meaning maps over a premise (`fmapM`) where it used bind-then-
    return, and `ana-semᵛ` returns the stream VALUE (building emits nothing), wrapped by the
    rule. `Effects`' monad laws are stated at grades with a unit; the graded laws through `toT∞`.
  - the unit as a premise: `returnM u`; the combinators, `lam`, `curry`, `ana` and a fold's
    algebra grade carry `u : pure ⊑π π`; the checker decides it (`withUnit`).
* C2b the surface's per-premise grades — MOVED TO F (D and E need no surface; the domain-given
  mode takes its grade as an input, so per-arm grades need grade synthesis there): `compose`/`pair` take a grade per arm and conclude at any grade above the
    product (`π₂ ·p π₁ ⊑π π`); `t-lam`'s free grade must be above `pure` (a lambda's body is
    pure). The checker synthesizes the LEAST grade of an arrow and checks it against the
    expected one (principal grades: `·p` is monotone, joins exist).

### F. Surface
**OPEN DECISION (user) — the surface names.** F needs keywords for the two new grades and for
the loop. Today an effectful arrow is `Eff A B` and an effectful stream `Nu (Eff F)`.
Recommended: `Act A B` for `A ⇒[eff⁺] B` (it acts — performs an event — before it answers),
`Run A B` for `A ⇒[prod] B` (it may run forever), `Nu (Act F)` / `Nu (Run F)` for the streams,
and `iter f` as a generator like `cata`/`ana` (`iter : Act A (B + A) -> Run A B`). The checker
work (C2b) does not depend on the names, but nothing in the surface can mention `eff⁺` until
they exist.
