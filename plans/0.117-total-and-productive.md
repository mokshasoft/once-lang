# Plan 0.117 — Once is total and productive, and a server is an ordinary function

## 0. Why

GOAL.md requires servers, and it asks termination only of PURE computations; of effects it asks
productivity. Under D311 `Eff A B` meant `A → T B` with `T` inductive, so an effectful program had
to stop — and a program that must stop is not truly productive (the user). D312: `Eff` is the free
completely iterative monad. A pure computation is a value (total); an effectful one reaches each
next event after finite work and need not stop (productive); `main : Eff Unit Unit` is a server as
well as a program that ends.

## 1. The mathematics (what a reader of the Spec should find)

* `T X` — the free monad on the call/halt signature `Σ`: the INITIAL algebra of `Y ↦ X + Σ Y`.
  Finite trees. It stays as the IR's finite denotation and as the shape of one guarded round.
* `T∞ X` — the free completely iterative monad: the FINAL coalgebra of the same functor; possibly
  infinite trees, no silent node. `Eff A B = A → T∞ B`. Equality is bisimilarity (D201).
* `T⁺ X` — the guarded computations: `T∞` trees whose root is an event (the ideal of `T∞`).
* Grades `pure ⊑ eff⁺ ⊑ eff`: `M pure X = X`, `M eff⁺ = T⁺`, `M eff = T∞`; an ordered monoid,
  sequencing concludes at the product of its premises' grades.
* Three structures, three formers, three universal properties: `μ` initial (`fold`/`cata`), `ν`
  final (`unfold`/`ana`), `T∞` completely iterative (guarded iteration `iter`): for a guarded step
  `f : A → B + T⁺ (B + A)` (end at once, or an event first), `iter f : A → T∞ B` is the unique
  solution of `x = f >=> [ret, x]` (up to bisimilarity); the Elgot laws follow from uniqueness.
* T+P: `M pure X = X`; every `T∞` tree is productive by the shape of its functor (no silent
  node); `Behavior n` of any program is computed by a total function of `n` (`events∞`).
