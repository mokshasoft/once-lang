# Plan 0.99 — Once has ONE subtyping judgment

## 0. The property

> **A term that types at `A` is accepted wherever `B` is expected exactly when
> there is a canonical, observation-free conversion `A → B` — and the program
> means the same thing whichever derivation found it.**

Stated once, as a judgment `A <: B` on types, used by one check-mode rule. Today
Once has one hard-wired instance (`t-subsume`, pure ⊑ eff) and needs a second
(`Void`, for 0.98 stage E: `exit : Eff Int Void` must be usable at
`main : Eff Unit Unit`). Adding the second as another standalone rule is how
special cases accumulate. This plan adds the structure instead.

## 1. What may enter `<:` — the admission criterion

A generator is admitted iff its conversion is (1) **canonical** — forced by the
structure, not chosen — and (2) **observation-free** — it can never lose
something the program could observe.

| conversion | canonical | observation-free | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Void <: B` — `¡` | unique (initial object; also initial in the Kleisli category) | vacuous: never runs | **admit** |
| grade `pure ⊑ eff` | the Freyd embedding (D068/D219) | grade is erased by `⟦_⟧` | **admit** (`t-subsume` becomes an instance) |
| `A <: Unit` — `!` | unique (terminal) | **erases a value**; breaks QTT linearity | reject |
| `Int <: Float` | chosen, not forced | not injective at fixed width | reject (D125 stays local) |

Uniqueness is necessary, not sufficient: `¡` is admissible because it is
VACUOUS, `!` is not because it ERASES.

## 2. The judgment — syntax-directed, so coherence is by construction

    sub-void : Void <: B
    sub-base : B <: B                        (B a base type other than Void)
    sub-arr  : A′ <: A → B <: B′ → π ⊑ π′
             → (A ⇒[ mk-kind q π ] B) <: (A′ ⇒[ mk-kind q π′ ] B′)
    sub-prod : A <: A′ → B <: B′ → (A * B) <: (A′ * B′)
    sub-sum  : A <: A′ → B <: B′ → (A + B) <: (A′ + B′)
    sub-μ    : μ-type F <: μ-type F           (reflexive only — see §6)
    sub-ν    : ν-type F <: ν-type F

* Domain CONTRAvariant, codomain covariant; the quantity `q` is NOT varied
  (sub-usage is a different axis — QTT's, not this plan's).
* **No transitivity rule.** Rules are disjoint on the head of the left type, so
  every `A <: B` has AT MOST ONE derivation: `<:-unique : (p q : A <: B) → p ≡ q`.
  That IS the coherence theorem (Reynolds / Curien–Ghelli) — true by
  construction instead of proved over a free relation. Transitivity and
  reflexivity are ADMISSIBLE lemmas.
* Decidable: `_<:?_ : (A B : Type) → Dec (A <: B)`.
* Every premise is a judgment on TYPES, never a computation on `RawExpr`
  (0.94 §2).

The motivating program needs congruence, not just the generator:
`exit@S (g 42) : Unit ⇒[eff] Void` checks at `Unit ⇒[eff] Unit` by
`sub-arr (sub-base) (sub-void) refl`. A codomain-only rule would not reach a
curried or nested `exit`.

## 3. The rule and its meaning

    t-sub : ctx ⊢ᶜ e ∶ A ⨾ Ψ → A <: B → ctx ⊢ᶜ e ∶ B ⨾ Ψ          (CHECK mode only)

replacing `t-subsume`. Inference is untouched: `⊢ᵢ` reports the least
(principal) type; D125's ruling — "Subsumption belongs in CHECK mode" — is kept.

Meaning: each derivation denotes a conversion `⟦ p ⟧<: : ⟦ A ⟧ᴰ → ⟦ B ⟧ᴰ`,
defined by recursion on `p`:

    ⟦ sub-void ⟧<:       = λ ()
    ⟦ sub-base ⟧<:       = id
    ⟦ sub-arr a b _ ⟧<:  = λ f x → fmapT ⟦ b ⟧<: (f (⟦ a ⟧<: x))   -- grade erased
    ⟦ sub-prod a b ⟧<:   = map× ⟦ a ⟧<: ⟦ b ⟧<:            (sum likewise)

`⟦ t-sub d p ⟧ᶜ = ⟦ p ⟧<: ∘ ⟦ d ⟧ᶜ`. At the pure⊑eff instance this is the
identity, exactly as `t-subsume` is today.

## 4. The layers it touches

| layer | change | where |
|---|---|---|
| Spec types | `_<:_`, `_<:?_`, `<:-unique`, `<:-refl`, `<:-trans` | new `Once/Type/Sub.agda`, re-exported by `Once.Spec.Type` |
| Spec typing | `t-sub` replaces `t-subsume` | `TypeCheck/Judgment` (= `Spec.Typing`) |
| Spec meaning | `⟦ p ⟧<:`, the `t-sub` clause | `Denotation/Meaning` |
| Surface | `sub p e` generalises `arr'` | `Surface/Syntax`, `SourceDenote`, `Surface/Elaborate` (IR: identity, or post-compose `initial` under the arrow) |
| realize | `realize (t-sub d p) = sub p (realize d)` | `Denotation/Realize` |
| Elaborator | check-mode fallback: infer `A`, decide `A <:? T`, wrap. The 6 `t-subsume` sites become `t-sub … (pure⊑eff instance)` | `TypeCheck/Elaborate` |
| Proofs | soundness (free), `check-complete` (`subsume-complete` → `sub-complete`), `bridge-c` (`RelV` respects `⟦ p ⟧<:`), `RealizeAgrees`, `ElaborateProofs` | `Completeness`, `MeaningBridge`, `RealizeAgrees` |

`realize-invariant` (A4) is still a postulate; `<:-unique` is part of what its
eventual discharge needs, and nothing here makes it less true.

## 5. Phases

**A. POC — before any Spec edit** (0.94 §4's own instruction).
   `Once/Type/Sub.agda` standalone: the relation, `_<:?_`, `<:-unique`,
   admissible `<:-trans`, and `⟦_⟧<:`. Then the 0.94 §4 case: `compose f g`
   with `g` ending in `Void` has a derivation at every middle type; show the
   meanings coincide (via `<:-unique` + `⟦_⟧<:`) and the principal choice
   (`Void`) is what inference yields. Gate: all green, no NEW postulates.
   **DONE**: `Once/Type/{DecEq,Sub}.agda`, `Once/Denotation/Sub.agda` —
   `<:-unique`, `_<:?_`, `<:-refl-id` (reflexivity means the identity),
   `<:-trans-∘` (transitivity means composition), and `void-middle`: the
   composite through `Void` does not depend on the middle type OR on `f`. Only
   axiom used: the existing `extensionality`.

**B. Spec.** `t-sub` replaces `t-subsume` in the judgment; the meaning clause.
   Follow the red through `Meaning`, `Realize`, `SourceDenote`,
   `Surface/Elaborate` with `sub p` generalising `arr'`.

**C. Elaborator.** The general check-mode fallback (`A <:? T`), and the six
   `t-subsume` sites re-expressed. `ElaborateProofs`/soundness follow.

**D. Proofs.** `check-complete` (the real work: the elaborator must FIND a
   `t-sub` derivation wherever the judgment has one), `bridge-c`,
   `RealizeAgrees`. Apex (`Certified`) green on all three arches.

**E. Hand back to 0.98 stage E**: `exit`/`exit0` become `Eff … Void`, the
   `! halts` grammar and the `SigEffect` table are deleted. With `<:` in place
   the 95 `main = exit@S (…)` programs keep type-checking unchanged.

**F. Extraction gate** — once, after 0.98 E, standalone (no Claude session).

## 6. Deliberately out of scope

* Variance under `μ`/`ν`: needs functor variance (`F` positive in its
  argument). Reflexive only until a program wants it.
* Sub-usage (QTT `q ≤ q′`): a separate axis with its own linearity story.
* `Int <: Float`: excluded by §1, not deferred.

## 7. Risks

* **Completeness is the cost centre.** Every place the elaborator currently
  special-cases `t-subsume` must be re-derived through `A <:? T`. Measure after
  phase C before promising phase D's size.
* **Surface `sub p` at IR.** The pure⊑eff part is erased (as `arr'` is); the
  `Void` codomain needs `initial` post-composed under a closure. If that shape
  fights the existing IR proofs, fall back to eliminating `sub` by a derived
  Surface term (λ/app) — decide in phase B, not before.
