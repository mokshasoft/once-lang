## Tier 1 — mechanical clause-collapse + pragma removal

### A1 — eff-mode switch `iFromInferEff` (Completeness.agda ~1799–1845)

`subsume-complete (t-embed d@(t-X …)) = … embedOrSubsume-lifts / checkElab-fallback-*-eff`

`iFromInferEff : ctx ⊢ᵢ e ∶ A → ∃… checkElab ctx e (…eff…) ≡ success …`, move the 12
bodies in, and reduce the caller to one clause `subsume-complete (t-embed d) =
iFromInferEff d`. No pair intro-form appears (arrow-typed only) → no re-wrap. The
`t-arg-driven-app-check` arm still routes to `completeness-gap-arg-driven-app-check-eff`
(that postulate is a SEPARATE difficulty, out of scope here — do not try to discharge).

### A2 — fold the `t-apply-check` re-wrap into A1 (Completeness.agda ~1847)
`subsume-complete (t-apply-check d) = … infer-complete (t-apply-app-infer d) …`
reconstructs a DIFFERENT constructor around the destructured `d` (a genuine re-wrap,
currently tolerated because it is not a cycle). Unify on the shared apply premise so
`t-apply-check` and `t-apply-app-infer` handling take `d` directly (no re-wrap).

### A3 — collapse CanonReflectMutual RApp fan-out (~70 → ~4)

### B1 — poly-transport `TERMINATING` → well-founded `Acc` (removes 2 pragmas)
