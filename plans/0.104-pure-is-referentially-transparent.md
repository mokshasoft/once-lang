# Plan 0.104 — `pure` is referential transparency; ex falso is explicit

Closes plan 0.103's two open forks (G, 6e) by the decisions they turned out to need:
**D250** (the meaning is graded: a pure term denotes a value) and **D251** (ex falso is the
explicit eliminator `initial`, the CCC's `¡`; usage is syntactic). Both are Spec changes, made top-down: the
Spec first, then the red is followed through the compiler and the proofs.

## 0. The properties

* **Referential transparency (D250).** A pure derivation denotes a VALUE:
  `⟦ Γ ⊢[ Ψ ] t ∷ A ! pure ⟧ : Env Γ Ψ → ⟦A⟧ᴰ`. A pure arrow is a total function. A module
  entry (always pure) denotes a value, so referencing it IS its value.
* **Substitution (D251).** Typing is closed under kinded type substitution, exactly in type
  and usage, by a structural map (`poly-typed-at`). Its semantic twin is parametricity
  (`poly-instance-sem`).

## 1. Where things stand (2026-10-01)

* Every surface term elaborates to a PURE core term (`Spec.Elaboration.Elab` is
  `Γ ⊢[ Ψ ] t ∷ A ! pure`). Effects happen only when a suspension runs (D018), and `main` is
  `Unit ⇒[eff] Unit`. So the surface is already referentially transparent. Only the semantics
  failed to say so.
* The IR is ungraded and its arrows are `eff` (`⌈ A ⇛ B ⌉ = ⌈A⌉ ⇒[effK] ⌈B⌉`). The IR
  evaluator, the backend and the machine correspondences do not change. The bridges from the
  Spec meaning to the IR meaning gain the embedding `returnT` at pure points.

## 2. Phases

**A. The Spec (D250).**
* A.1 `Denotation.ValueDomain`: `M : Purity → Set → Set` (`M pure = id`, `M eff = T`). Arrows
  become `⟦A⟧ᴰ → M π ⟦B⟧ᴰ` (no argument at `Zero`, per D143), and `ν-type F π` becomes ν of
  `M π ∘ ⟦F⟧`.
* A.2 `Semantics.Value` (the contract domain): pure arrows are total (`⟦A⟧ → ⟦B⟧`); `eff`
  arrows keep `Res`.
* A.3 `Spec.Core.Meaning`: graded clauses. `⊢sub-eff` is `returnT`, and `⊢absurd` at pure is
  `⊥-elim`.
* A.4 `Spec.Core.Telescope`: `DefSem` gives each entry's VALUE, and the program meaning runs
  `main`'s value.
* A.5 The surface direct meaning (`Denotation.Meaning`) follows: a surface term denotes a
  value.

**B. The Spec (D251).**
* B.1 `TypeCheck.Judgment`: delete the 13 rules that make other formers Void-sensitive
  (`t-neg-void`, `t-case-void`, `t-binop-void-l/r`, `t-fst/snd/apply/Out-app-void`,
  `t-app-void`, `d-fst/snd/case/cata-void`). The explicit ex falso already exists:
  `initial e` (`t-initial-app-check`) and `d-initial`.
* B.2 `Spec.Elaboration`: the deleted rules' clauses go.
* B.3 Red tests first in `TypeCheckSpec`: `x + v` with `v ∶ Void` and a non-`Int` `x` is
  rejected, and `initial v` is accepted at any type.

**C. The compiler.** The elaborator (`TypeCheck.Elaborate`) drops the Void dispatch. `resolveExpr`/`realize` follow.

**D. The proofs, following the red.** Completeness, soundness, ModeAgreement, ErrorProofs,
RealizeAgrees, CoreMeaningBridge, TeleWalk (where `core-pure` is deleted), SourceDenote and the
bridges to `evalᴰ` (the `returnT` embedding).

**E. `poly-typed-at` as a structural map**, and `poly-instance-sem` by parametricity.

*Gate:* the Certified apex green; `make postulates` shows `core-pure`, `poly-typed-at` and
`poly-instance-sem` gone; MAlonzo re-extracted; `cabal test` green.

**Order.** B, C and D's frontend part first (D251 does not touch the value domain). Then A,
whose consequence is below.

**A's design (2026-10-01, from reading the apex chain).** The chain is

    core ⟦_⟧  ≡ (CoreMeaningBridge)  surface ⟦_⟧ᵢ  ~RelT~ (MeaningBridge)  SD.⟦ realize _ ⟧ˢ  ≡ (SourceFaithful, cohᴰ)  evalᴰ

MeaningBridge is ALREADY the logical relation between the Spec side and the implementation
side. So the cut is there:

* the SPEC layers (the core meaning, the surface direct meaning, the telescope) move to a
  GRADED domain `⟦_⟧ᵛ`, with `M π`;
* SD and the IR keep the Kleisli domain `⟦_⟧ᴰ` (purity-blind, as an implementation model may
  be). `cohᴰ` and its ~500 uses stay valid: SourceFaithful relates two Kleisli meanings;
* `RelV`/`RelT` become heterogeneous, `RelV A : ⟦A⟧ᵛ → ⟦A⟧ᴰ → Set`. The new clause shapes:
  * a pure arrow: `RelV (A ⇒[ q , pure ] B) f g = ∀ {a b} → RelV A a b → RelM pure B (f a) (g b)`,
    with `RelM pure B v m = RelT B (returnT v) m` (the Kleisli side returns silently a related
    value) and `RelM eff = RelT`;
  * `ν-type F pure`: a heterogeneous bisimulation between plain codata and `νᵈ`;
* the semantic operations the Spec shares with SD (`cata-sem`, `ana-sem`, `out-sem`,
  `sigOpRefᴰ`, `Phase`'s environment operations) get graded twins, or are parameterised by the
  domain where they are grade-agnostic (`Phase`).

This replaces "`cohᴰ` → `RelD`" below: no transport is lost, because the transport was only
ever between two Kleisli meanings. The graded↔Kleisli adequacy is the one relation that
already exists.

**A, order.** A.1 `⟦_⟧ᵛ` + `M` (and `νᵖ`, the pure final coalgebra); A.2 `Semantics.Value`'s
pure arrows; A.3 `Spec.Core.Meaning` graded; A.4 `Telescope` (`DefSem` = values); A.5 the
surface direct meaning; A.6 `MeaningRelation` heterogeneous, then MeaningBridge,
CoreMeaningBridge and TeleWalk/TeleEntry (`core-pure` deleted). SD, SourceFaithful, the IR and
the backend do not change.

## 3. Risks

* A.1 changes the value of every pure arrow, so every proof that relates a surface or core
  meaning at an arrow type (the logical relations `RelT`/`RelV`, `abi-rel`, `Agree`) changes
  at that clause. The relation at a pure arrow becomes "related functions", with no `T`.
* ν at `pure` needs a coinductive (or reified) final coalgebra of `⟦F⟧` in Set. If the
  existing `νᵈ` representation cannot carry it, A.1's ν row is staged after the arrows.
