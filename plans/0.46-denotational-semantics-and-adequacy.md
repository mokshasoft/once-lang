# Plan 0.46 — Denotational (monadic) semantics + adequacy

> **One sentence.** Give Once a **compositional denotational meaning**
> `⟦_⟧ᴰ` (an effect-graded monadic semantics, proof-side only), and prove it
> **adequate** to the operational trace `obs` — so we can reason *equationally*
> about Once programs and certify the optimizer, while the operational `obs`
> remains the ground-truth observable the machine refines.

## Status / sequencing

**This plan is DEFERRED behind the operational `obs` redesign (solution 2).**
The operational `obs` *defines the observable* (the syscall trace is operational
by nature); it is what `flat-events` refines and what `#10` connects to
`SS.eval`. `⟦_⟧ᴰ` is a *reasoning layer proven adequate to it*, so the
operational semantics must exist first. Build `⟦_⟧ᴰ` + adequacy when its first
real consumer is concrete — the certified optimizer (**Plan 0.39**) — not before
(a denotational semantics is designed best once the equivalences it must prove
are known). This is dependency ordering, **not** scope-deferral: the optimizer
*will* be certified, so `⟦_⟧ᴰ` is future-certain, not optional.

## Why a denotational semantics at all

The operational `obs` answers "what does running this emit?". It is **not
compositional**: there's no clean `⟦g ∘ f⟧ = ⟦g⟧ ⊙ ⟦f⟧` law to rewrite with.
Two distinct needs require compositional equational reasoning `⟦e₁⟧ᴰ = ⟦e₂⟧ᴰ`:

1. **Certifying the optimizer (Plan 0.39).** `optimize` applies categorical
   rewrites — beta/eta, fold/unfold fusion, map fusion. These are *equations*.
   Proving each preserves meaning is cleanest as `⟦optimize ir⟧ᴰ = ⟦ir⟧ᴰ`
   (equational, by the CCC + monad laws), versus an operational simulation per
   rewrite. Adequacy then lifts this to "optimize preserves the observable
   trace", admitting `optimize` to the certified pipeline.

2. **Reasoning about Once PROGRAMS, not just the compiler.** *(This is the
   deeper reason — flagged by the user 2026-06-14.)* A verified compiler tells
   you the binary matches the source's meaning. It does **not** let you prove
   anything *about* that meaning. Compositional `⟦_⟧ᴰ` is what lets a user (or a
   tool, or a downstream proof) establish program-level properties and
   equivalences: that two implementations of `main` are observationally equal,
   that a refactor preserves behaviour, that a library function satisfies its
   spec, that an effect is never emitted on some path, etc. — by equational
   reasoning at the source/IR level, *independent of any particular run*.
   Without a denotational semantics, Once has a *certified compiler* but no
   *program logic*; programs can only be reasoned about by executing them.
   `⟦_⟧ᴰ` (with adequacy back to the observable) is the foundation of an
   eventual Once program logic / equational theory. This makes `⟦_⟧ᴰ` valuable
   even if the optimizer never shipped.

## The semantics (proof-side, effect-graded, arrow-respecting)

The monad **never surfaces in Once**. Once stays a CCC + structured recursion
built on arrows; effects are tracked at the **arrow kind** (`⇒[ k ]`, `k`
carrying purity). `⟦_⟧ᴰ` *interprets* those kinds:

- A pure arrow `A ⇒[ Many , pure ] B`  ↦  `⟦A⟧ → ⟦B⟧`.
- An effectful arrow `A ⇒[ Many , eff ] B`  ↦  the Kleisli arrow `⟦A⟧ → T ⟦B⟧`,
  where `T` is the trace monad (a fuel-indexed Writer over `List SigOpEvent`,
  fuel for productive `Ana`; finite `Writer` suffices for `Cata`).
- `SigOp si` ↦ `tell` its event (for `Emits`/`Halts`; `Pure` SigOps `tell []`,
  per the effect-aware `obs`/`flat-events`, Plan 0.45).
- `_∘_` ↦ Kleisli composition; `⟨_,_⟩`, `case`, `Cata` ↦ the standard monadic
  combinators (`Cata` = a structural fold whose algebra runs in `T`).

So the monad is **effect-graded** (the arrow kind decides pure-fn vs Kleisli),
purely metatheoretic, and respects the existing effect discipline:
`Eff`-arrows ↦ Kleisli arrows of `T`. It appears in `⟦_⟧ᴰ` only — not in the
IR, the surface syntax, or any user-written type. Higher-order effects are
handled *compositionally* (`⟦apply⟧(clo,a) = clo a` runs the closure's Kleisli
arrow, telling its events) — which is exactly the gap that the operational
redesign fixes operationally.

## Adequacy

The bridge: **the trace of `⟦ir⟧ᴰ` equals the operational `obs ir`** (on each
input, up to the event-prefix / fuel indexing). Concretely
`projTrace (⟦ ir ⟧ᴰ x) ≡ proj₁ (obs n ir x)` at matching fuel.

Reuse: the cata correctness already being built — *machine loop ≈ denotational
`sem-cata` fold* — **is literally an adequacy instance** (operational ≈
denotational for the fold). So the denotational cata machinery
(`cata-nat-obs-fold`, `cata-ev-alg`, `sem-cata`) is not thrown away by going
operational first; it re-enters here as the cata case of adequacy.

## Milestones (when activated)

- **M1.** Define `T` (fuel-Writer) + the effect-graded interpretation of arrow
  kinds; `⟦_⟧ᴰ : IR A B → ⟦A⟧ → T ⟦B⟧` (Kleisli), compositional.
- **M2.** Monad + CCC laws for `⟦_⟧ᴰ` (the equational toolkit): `⟦id⟧`,
  `⟦g∘f⟧ = ⟦g⟧ ∘ₖ ⟦f⟧`, beta/eta, the fusion laws the optimizer needs.
- **M3.** Adequacy `projTrace ∘ ⟦_⟧ᴰ ≈ obs` (operational), with the cata case
  reusing the loop≈fold work.
- **M4.** Optimizer correctness (Plan 0.39): `⟦optimize ir⟧ᴰ = ⟦ir⟧ᴰ`; lift via
  M3 to trace-preservation; admit `optimize` (doOpt = true) to the certified
  pipeline.
- **M5 (future / research).** Reasoning interface for Once programs — a small
  equational program logic on top of `⟦_⟧ᴰ` (observational equivalence,
  effect-freedom on a path, spec satisfaction).

## Dependent-types caveat

A *monadic denotational* model of a **dependently-typed effectful** language is
research-frontier (graded/indexed monads compatible with type dependency, or an
LCCC + monad model). The **operational** semantics (solution 2) extends to
dependent types far more readily and is therefore the robust long-term
foundation; `⟦_⟧ᴰ` is the compositional reasoning layer layered on top via
adequacy. If/when Once gains dependent types, revisit whether `⟦_⟧ᴰ` follows the
graded-monad route or is replaced by an operational program logic.
