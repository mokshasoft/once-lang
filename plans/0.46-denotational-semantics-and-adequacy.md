# Plan 0.46 / OCP-0006 — One typed, total+productive semantics (no islands)

> **One sentence.** Once has **one** meaning — a typed, **fuel-free**,
> total+productive denotational trace semantics `⟦_⟧ˢ` over the
> **intrinsically-typed surface `Expr`** — and the IR denotation `⟦_⟧ᴰ`, the
> machine, and every compiler stage are **derived from it or proven against it**;
> the untyped, fuel-bounded `SS.eval` island is **retired**; and **Agda's own
> totality/productivity checker is the TP invariant**, in force everywhere because
> there is no fuel and no untyped layer left to switch it off.

---

## ⚠️ THIS IS A TOP-DOWN ENDEAVOUR — NOT MIDDLE-OUT ⚠️ (read first, twice)

Every recurring debacle in this work — the general-recursion leak, the
`CompSim`/`ProdSim` split, the finite-vs-productive confusion, the
`prefix-take`/`functor-walk` lemmas built before anything demanded them — has the
**same cause**: we built **islands** (locally-coherent definitions/lemmas/relations)
**before the top forced them to exist**, and they didn't match at integration time.

So this plan is executed **strictly top-down**, and the discipline is mechanical,
not aspirational:

1. **The apex is the only root.** `⟦src⟧ = ⟦mainExpr⟧ˢ`. Everything else is either
   a **hole in the apex's proof tree** (discovered, not invented) or a **derived
   view** of `⟦_⟧ˢ` proven against it. **An exported lemma the apex never
   transitively calls is an island — delete it or connect it.**
2. **No free-standing relations or lemmas.** Do not define a relation (à la
   `CompSim`/`ProdSim`) and then look for a use. State the apex, attempt it, and
   let the *holes* dictate the single relation actually needed.
3. **No fuel. Anywhere. In the meaning.** Fuel is the escape hatch that turns a
   partial computation total and thereby **disables Agda's TP checker** — it is
   how general recursion leaked in. `ℕ` is permitted **only** as
   *event-observation depth* (consumed solely by `Ana`), never as a termination
   device. A fuel parameter in a semantics signature is a bug.
4. **Definition-first when re-rooting.** When the apex changes shape, commit the
   new (possibly red) statement first so the gap is explicit; then fix downward.
   Never weaken the apex to fit an existing lemma (THE RULE, from 0.44).

If you catch yourself proving something the apex did not ask for, **stop** — that
is the middle-out reflex, and it is the thing this plan exists to prevent.

## Why this plan was rewritten (the islands, named)

The 2026-06-14 version had **two** semantics by design: `⟦_⟧ᴰ` (the clean,
fuel-free IR denotation) as the spec, **and** `SS.eval` (an *independent, untyped,
fuel-bounded* `RawExpr` interpreter, D057) as a load-bearing cross-check, tied by
`SS.eval ≡ ⟦_⟧ᴰ` (#10). That two-semantics shape **is** the island structure:

- `SS.eval` is over **untyped `RawExpr`**, so it has no totality and needs **fuel**;
  fuel re-admits **general recursion** (a self-recursive def recurses through
  `lookupDef`, fuel-bounded) — exactly what OCP-0003 removed from the IR. The
  reference drifted from the language's total+productive discipline, and the
  mismatch surfaced (repeatedly) only at integration.
- Cross-checking one hand-written interpreter against another buys no trust — it
  buys a second thing that can be wrong, and did.

The fix is **not** to derive `SS.eval` from the elaborator (that would re-bake the
elaborator into the spec — the IR-pivot 0.45 correctly rejected, which would make
the typechecker un-catchable). The fix is to notice that `SS.eval` was
**conflating two jobs**, and to split them so neither needs an untyped trace.

## The decision (OCP-0006 — encoded as Agda invariants, not a doc)

**The typechecker's correctness is two separate properties, and neither is a trace
property:**

1. **Soundness (output is well-typed).** `Expr` is **intrinsically typed**
   (`Expr Γ Ψ A`). `checkElab : RawExpr → Maybe (typed Expr)` therefore *cannot*
   produce an ill-typed term — Agda rejects any definition that would. The
   typechecker's soundness is **load-bearing by construction**, encoded in the
   `Expr` type itself. (This is the OCP-0006 invariant for the typechecker: a
   wrong typing is a type error, caught by Agda, not by a runtime trace.)
2. **Faithfulness (output is the *right* term for the source).** The **syntactic
   `erase` round-trip** `erase (checkElab raw) ≡ raw` (modulo canonical names). A
   `checkElab` that produces a well-typed-but-wrong term breaks this. Syntactic,
   fuel-free — and it is exactly the `erase`/α machinery already built.

So the trace meaning and the typechecker-faithfulness are **decoupled**:

- **Trace meaning → `⟦_⟧ˢ` over the typed `Expr`** — total+productive by
  construction (no fuel; Agda's checker certifies it). **The single anchor.**
- **Typechecker faithfulness → the `erase` round-trip** (syntactic, already built).
- **Elaborator → meaning preservation:** `⟦ elaborate e ⟧ᴰ ≡ ⟦ e ⟧ˢ`. The source
  meaning is independent of `elaborate`, so a meaning-changing elaboration breaks
  this — **elaborator stays load-bearing.**
- **Machine → refinement:** `flat-events … ≡ proj₁ (⟦ ir ⟧ᴰ …)`.

Both stages stay load-bearing — the typechecker by intrinsic typing + `erase`, the
elaborator by meaning preservation — which is the *correct* way to verify each
(typing/syntax for the typechecker, meaning for the elaborator). `SS.eval` is
**retired**: its trace job moves to `⟦_⟧ˢ`, its faithfulness job to `erase`.

## Architecture (one root; everything derived or proven against it)

```
                         RawExpr  (parsed surface text)
                            │  checkElab
                            │      ├── soundness:    intrinsic typing  (by construction)
                            │      └── faithfulness: erase (checkElab raw) ≡ raw  (syntactic)
                            ▼
   ⟦_⟧ˢ : Expr → T   ◀── THE SEMANTICS (typed, fuel-free, total+productive)   = ⟦src⟧
                            │  elaborate
                            │      └── ⟦ elaborate e ⟧ᴰ ≡ ⟦ e ⟧ˢ   (elaborator load-bearing)
                            ▼
   ⟦_⟧ᴰ : IR → T     ◀── derived view (same monad T; provably = ⟦_⟧ˢ via elaborate)
                            │  codegen / assemble
                            │      └── flat-events … ≡ proj₁ (⟦ ir ⟧ᴰ …)   (machine refines)
                            ▼
                         machine bytes  →  exec
```

- **`T X = ℕ → List SigOpEvent × X`** (budget-indexed Writer). The `ℕ` is
  **event-observation depth** (D058), consumed **only** by `Ana`; it is **not**
  fuel. `⟦_⟧ˢ`/`⟦_⟧ᴰ` terminate by **structural recursion on the syntax** —
  Agda certifies totality, productivity is the `Ana` depth.
- **`⟦A ⇒[k] B⟧ = ⟦A⟧ → T ⟦B⟧`** (effect-carrying arrow) — higher-order effects
  compositional and fuel-free (the 0.46 insight, retained).
- `⟦_⟧ˢ` and `⟦_⟧ᴰ` share the **same target `T`**; they are two structural folds
  (surface vs CCC) into one monad, tied by `elaborate`. **Not** two independent
  semantics — one meaning, two syntaxes, one proven bridge.

## Milestones (TOP-DOWN; re-root first, derive downward)

- **M1 — Define `⟦_⟧ˢ : Expr → T` over the intrinsically-typed surface.**
  Total, fuel-free, structural on `Expr`; arrows effect-carrying; `Cata` the
  structural fold, `Ana` the depth-bounded unfold (the sole `T`-`ℕ` consumer).
  Reuse the existing `T`/`⟦_⟧ᴰ` monad machinery. Additive + green; nothing
  downstream changes yet. **This is the new root.**
- **M2 — Re-root the apex on `⟦_⟧ˢ`.** `⟦src⟧ = ⟦ mainExpr ⟧ˢ`. Restate
  `compiled-main-trace`/`elaborate-trace-correct` as the elaborator obligation
  `⟦ elaborate e ⟧ᴰ ≡ ⟦ e ⟧ˢ` (depth-`k` prefix). Definition-first: commit the
  re-rooted statement, then discharge downward.
- **M3 — Discharge `⟦ elaborate e ⟧ᴰ ≡ ⟦ e ⟧ˢ` top-down.** Structural induction on
  `Expr`; each constructor a hole the top demands. `Cata`/`Ana` cases are where
  the recursion-scheme correspondence lives — **no fuel, no monotonicity, no
  `functor-walk` unless a hole asks for it.**
- **M4 — Retire `SS.eval` / `RawExpr`-trace / fuel.** Delete the trace role of
  `Once.Verified.SourceSemantics` (`runTrace`, `anaUnfold`, `mapAnaF`, the fuel).
  Keep `erase` + the round-trip as the **syntactic** typechecker-faithfulness
  lemma, decoupled from the trace. Excise the `CompSim`/`ProdSim`/`prefix-take`/
  `functor-walk`/`ana-trace-*` islands the re-rooted top no longer calls.
- **M5 — Machine refines `⟦_⟧ᴰ`.** `flat-events … ≡ proj₁ (⟦ir⟧ᴰ …)`; no
  `enough-fuel`/`run-halts` (depth, not completion).
- **M6 — Optimizer (Plan 0.39):** `⟦optimize ir⟧ᴰ = ⟦ir⟧ᴰ` ⇒ `doOpt = true`.

## What this retires (the islands)

- `SS.eval` as a trace semantics (untyped, fuel) — **retired**; trace → `⟦_⟧ˢ`.
- `RawExpr`-level trace, `runTrace`, `anaUnfold`/`mapAnaF`, **all termination fuel**.
- `CompSim`/`ProdSim` as *free-standing relations*, and the `ana-trace-*` /
  `functor-walk` / `take-*` / `prefix-take` lemmas built bottom-up — kept **only**
  if a hole in M3 transitively demands them; otherwise deleted as islands.

Kept: the `T` monad + `⟦_⟧ᴰ` machinery (M1c), `erase` + the round-trip (now a
syntactic typechecker lemma), the machine/codegen refinement work.
