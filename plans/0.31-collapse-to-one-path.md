# Plan 0.31 — Collapse to one path (live == proven)

## Root cause of the mess

**We prove `compile-ir` but ship `ir-to-trace` — two different compilers.**
Every symptom flows from this split:
- `Correct.agda` (×3 targets) → `EntryPointCCC` (`compile = compile-ir`) →
  `compile-ir`. The verified correctness theorem is about `compile-ir`.
- The binary runs `Once.Compile → Target.X86-64 → ir-to-trace →
  compile-trace-cnt` (the live path).
- 3 parallel Cata structures: `compile-ir` (dead, proven), `ir-to-trace`
  (live, fold wired in Plan 0.29 M5), `CataLayer` process-layer (abstract
  validity, general over F). Plus our standalone operational proofs
  (`AbstractCataFoldInduction`, `CataDescendCompiledInduction`).
- `DirectSimulation` is the abstract↔target bridge but its target model
  `exec-prog` is STRAIGHT-LINE — it cannot represent a loop back-edge, so
  the abstract machine's new `instr-loop`/`case-on-tag` have no honest
  per-instruction `Corresponds` (the `!!` holes). The loop-aware target
  model that DOES exist is `Semantics.exec` (`find-label`).

## The fix: one path, live and proven

1. Keep `ir-to-trace` (live; folds Cata for real).
2. Re-base the correctness proofs onto it; retire `compile-ir` +
   `EntryPointCCC`'s dead entry.
3. The re-based proof's target model must handle loops → `Semantics.exec`,
   with leaf instructions corresponded per-instruction and
   `instr-loop`/`case-on-tag` by TRACE-LEVEL FUEL INDUCTION
   (= `CataDescendCompiledInduction` shape, made load-bearing).

## Open design question for Phase 1

Does the re-based bridge REPLACE `DirectSimulation`/`exec-prog`, or sit
alongside it (straight-line IRs keep `exec-prog`, control-flow IRs use the
new `Semantics.exec` bridge)? Leaning: one model (`Semantics.exec`) for
everything — straight-line is the degenerate (no-jump) case — to avoid a
4th parallel structure. Decide before 1a.
