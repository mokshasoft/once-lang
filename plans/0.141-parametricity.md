# Plan 0.141 — Polymorphic definitions are parametric, and so natural

## Summary

A definition that works for every type, such as reversing a list, cannot look at the elements it moves
around. So reversing and then changing every element gives the same list as changing every element and
then reversing. Mathematicians call this naturality; it is one of the reasoning principles Once promises,
and the only one the language does not state yet. This plan states the general principle behind it (a
polymorphic definition treats every type the same way, "parametricity") as part of the language
definition, proves it with no axioms, and derives naturality from it.

## 1. Why

GOAL.md promises "equational reasoning, naturality, fold fusion, the laws of the effect structure". Plan
0.79 stated and proved the rest (D369); naturality was split off because it needs new machinery:
every D369 law relates one judgment to itself, at one instance, while naturality relates a definition
at two instances, `r_A` and `r_B`.

## 2. Where we are (2026-11-03)

- `Same` (`Once.Spec.Equivalence`) relates a type's values to themselves; the laws (`_≅_`,
  `Once.Spec.Metatheory`) quantify over definitions' meanings `δ` with `SameDefs δ`: each instance of a
  definition is `Same` to itself. Instances at different types are unrelated, so naturality is not
  provable under `SameDefs` (it is false for an arbitrary `δ`); it holds for a program's own definitions
  (`Core.Program.teleSem`).
- `mapᶜ` (D369, plan 0.79 D4) is a type's action on effect-free arrows: the maps naturality is stated with.
- The fundamental lemma (`Once.Metatheory.Coherence`, `FL`) is the identity-relation case of the
  abstraction theorem this plan needs.

**Audit (step 1, done 2026-11-03): nothing in the core inspects a type variable.** Checked
`Once.Spec.Core.{Syntax,Typing,Meaning}`, `Once.Spec.{Domain,Sub,Signature}`, the surface typing:
- Types in terms are annotations only (`lam`, `inl`, `inr`, `absurd`, `coerce`, functors of
  `roll`/`fold`/`unfold`); their meaning is uniform in a variable (`toL`/`fromL` at `K`, `conv` at
  `sub-var` is the identity).
- `lit`, `prim` (`primDom`/`primCod`) and `sigop` (`⌜ b ⌝ᵈ`, the signature's base types) are at closed
  types; there is no polymorphic primitive. The surface's comparisons (`Compare`) are `Int`/`Float` prims.
- Kinds (`base`, `any`) only restrict what a variable may be instantiated with (`Respects`); no rule
  computes with a kind.
- `ref d τ` is the one place an instance enters a meaning: `defs ρ d τ` — which is why the hypothesis on
  `δ` is the issue above, not a construct.
- The surface picks instances statically (`c-poly`, `d-poly`); that is elaboration, not a run-time test.

What the relational reading cannot reuse as is (checked 2026-11-03):
- `Same (μ F)` is `≡`, and a functor constant's layer equality (`LayerEq (SK B)`, `Spec.Effect`) is `≡`.
  Both are right for the identity relation (constants are base types, so trees are first-order data),
  but between two instances a tree's relation must be structural.
- Everything relating computations and layers is homogeneous: `Observed` (`Spec.Equivalence`),
  `_≈ᵗ⟨_⟩_`, `EndRel`, `StepRel` (`Spec.Trace`), `LayerEq`, `OneLayer`, `SameNu`. Between two instances
  the two sides have different value types, so each needs a heterogeneous form (`R : X → Y → Set`).
  Events are compared by `≡` in `StepRel`; that stays (operation arguments and answers are base types).
- `Same` is on closed types (`Type = Ty 0`), and the meaning of an open type is read through a
  substitution (`⟦ A ⟨ σ ⟩ ⟧ᵛ`). A closed `A ⟨ σ ⟩` is not `A` by computation, only up to a lemma. So
  `Same` cannot be "the relational reading at no variables" by definition without transporting along
  that lemma, which would stop `Same (A * B)` from computing to a product. See P1 for how the one
  definition is kept anyway.

## 3. Decisions

- **P1, one definition of sameness — DECIDED (user, 2026-11-03): the Spec does not define the same idea
  twice.** The relational reading of types (each type a relation between two instances, given a relation
  per type variable) goes in `Once.Spec.Equivalence`, and `Same` is its identity case, not a second
  definition proved equal.

  Design (2026-11-03, to confirm with the Spec patch): by §2, `Same` cannot be the open reading at zero
  variables by computation. The one definition is instead the **relational action of each type former**,
  defined once, heterogeneously: products (`R × S`), sums, graded arrows (related arguments to related
  computations, observed), `μ` (a tree relation built structurally from the layer relation), `ν`
  (agreement to every depth), and the observation of computations (`Observed`, traces, `End`).
  `Same : Type → …` and the open reading `Rel : Ty m → …` (given a relation per variable) are each the
  fold of a type with these formers; they differ only at variables, which a closed type has none of.
  Consequences: `Same (μ F)` and `LayerEq (SK B)` become structural instead of `≡` (the metatheory proves
  the `≡` forms as lemmas where used), and `Spec.Trace`/`Spec.Effect`'s relations become heterogeneous
  (the homogeneous ones their instances).
- **P2, the hypothesis on definitions — DECIDED (user, 2026-11-03): `ParamDefs δ`.** Definitions'
  meanings are related across instances (at related instances, by the relational reading of the
  definition's type); `SameDefs` becomes its identity case, not a separate definition. Uniform with every
  law; the metatheory proves `ParamDefs (teleSem …)` by induction along the telescope. Rejected: stating
  the theorem for a program's own definitions (`teleSem`) only.
- **P3, what is stated — DECIDED (user, 2026-11-03): the abstraction theorem**, with naturality for
  `∀a. F a → G a` as a derived law with `mapᶜ`. The theorem: a derivation's meanings at two instances
  related by a relation per variable, in related environments, under `ParamDefs`, are related by its
  type's relational reading. Rejected: naturality only (it says nothing about `(a → a) → a → a`).
- **P4, effects — DECIDED (user, 2026-11-03):** the definition may be effectful, the map `f` effect-free
  (`Pure`); computations are related by observation, as `SameM`.
- **P5, dependent types (OCP-0009, branch `origin/plan-ref`, read 2026-11-03).** That branch commits to
  nothing about parametricity, and its chosen kernel does not break it: `bootstrap/DirectedHoTT/Spec/Syntax.agda`
  has `U`/`El` and codes but no eliminator on `U` (`PLAN-BIDI.md`: "`U` has no eliminator, so codes cannot
  be cased on"). Two things bear on this plan:
  - the earlier PoC took `U = μ UF` as data (`poc/OCP0009/NbEPCwF.agda`) and OCP-0009 Rung 1 proposes
    "let `Cata` compute over *types*" (datatype-generic programming) — type-case, which breaks
    parametricity over `U` unless the code argument is erased;
  - the kernel deliberately does not carry naturality in its hom-types (`ARCHITECTURE.md` §G8, refuted at
    `U` by `SpikeHomNatU`), so naturality is an external theorem about definable terms, never a property
    the type theory supplies. QTT erasure (`NbEPQTT.erase-irrelevant`, PoC only) gives "a `𝟘` index does
    not affect the result", a stepping stone, not the relational statement.

  **DECIDED (user, 2026-11-03):** the theorem is stated externally, over derivations (as P3 does), and
  the principle is a language decision, recorded in the D-entry and `language.md`: **a type argument is
  parametric; a construct that cases on types (an eliminator on `U` codes, a type-indexed primitive) may
  only take them at grade `𝟘`, or must be a decision of its own.** Today's `∀a` maps to `(a :𝟘 U) → …` on
  that path. A future datatype-generic feature then has to go through this decision explicitly.
