# Plan 0.122 — Collapse the compiler's internal language onto the Spec (after the merge)

status: OPEN — all layers DONE 2026-10-26 (`Once.Internal` deleted, D336); left: the merge (written 2026-10-21,
at the user's request). Started 2026-10-22 on branch `collapse-internal`,
after the merge (master 3015eba09). Route chosen by the user 2026-10-22: §5.

## 0. Where this starts

Before the merge (plan 0.121 §5, the fast path the user chose):
- the theorem is the Spec's: `Once.Certified.once-compiler = SpecApex.spec-compiler SpecApex.bridges`
  (D332), over the Spec's source, typing, behaviour and Interpretations;
- the Spec's own definitions replace their copies (grades `Spec.Grade`, the effect order `⊑π`), the
  compiler's extras over them in `Once.Type.Grade`;
- what remained of `Once.Lang` — the compiler's internal language — lives under `Once.Internal`, as compiler
  code: types with rigid parameters, the checker's usage-tracking bidirectional judgment, the elaboration,
  the core it elaborates to, its meaning, contracts, deciders.
- The bridges (`Adequacy.SpecCore`, `SpecFront`, `SpecGrammar`, `SpecApex`) prove that internal language
  faithful to the Spec, both ways.

The theorem and its trust base do NOT change in this plan. What it buys: one language instead of two — the
checker and the codegen proved directly against the Spec, the bridges gone, less to maintain.

## 1. Size (measured 2026-10-21)

`Once.Lang` was 69 modules, 12,100 lines, imported by 350 of 693 modules (`Type` 278, `Type.Grade` 182,
`Type.WellFormed` 115, `Trace` 102, `Context` 90, `Contract` 76, `Syntax` 69, `Typing` 59, `Core.PolyTy` 61).
Every layer rests on the grades (done before the merge) and then the types.

Lesson from layer 1 (grades), before the merge: the cost is the type-checking loop, not the editing. Agda
stops at the first error and re-checks everything above the fixed module; with ~180 touched modules a run
takes 5–10 minutes and finds one error. Plan the layers so that one run surfaces many errors:
- scripted rewrites for each mechanical class of change, checked by grep before running Agda;
- check the lowest broken module directly (its dependencies are cached) before the apex;
- never invalidate interfaces needlessly (moving imports re-checks the whole cone);
- run on a quiet machine (the extraction and the apex check need ~5 GB; at most two Agda processes fit).

## 2. Layers (each committed green: apex `Once.Certified`, backstop, extraction + cabal + exit tests)

1. **Types.** `Internal.Type` (unscoped `rigid k i`) → `Spec.Type` (`Ty m`, `KCtx m`). The checker's context
   carries the current definition's type-variable count `m` and kinds; deciders (`DecEq`, `isConcrete?`,
   `honest?`, the join's computation, `Ground`/`extractGround`) become compiler code over `Ty m`. Subtyping
   and well-formedness from `Spec.Sub`/`Spec.Type`. The bridges' `TyR` becomes equality; delete it.
2. **Core.** `Internal.Core.*` (`Syntax`, `Typing`, `PolyTy`, `PolyTyping`, `Translate`, `Abstract`,
   `Schema`, `Derived`, `Rename`, `Subst`, `TySubst`) → `Spec.Core.*`: the elaborator produces Spec core
   terms; the IR lowering reads them. `SpecCore.Term`/`TermR`/`Poly.trD` disappear.
3. **Domains and meaning.** `Internal.Graded`/`Value`/`Trace`/`Contract`/`Event`/`Iterative`/`Stream`/
   `Meaning`/`FunctorMeaning` → `Spec.Domain`/`Effect`/`Observation`/`Core.Meaning`. The codegen's
   correctness (`CCC/Codegen/IRObsCorrect`, `Adequacy`, `Denotation`) restated over the Spec's meaning; the
   meaning relation (`SpecCore.Fundamental`, `MeaningBridge`) disappears. Largest layer.
4. **Surface.** `Internal.Syntax`/`Resolution`/`Typing`/`Elaboration`/`Module`/`Program`/`Parsing`/
   `Grammar`/`Lexing` → the Spec's surface: the parser produces `Spec.Surface.Syntax` (the `Emb`/`DEmb`
   relations become identity), the resolver implements `Spec.Surface.Resolution`, the checker is verified
   against `Spec.Surface.Typing` plus the core's usage, and `ModuleTyped` is the Spec's `Denotes`.
   `SpecFront` disappears; `SpecApex.bridges` becomes immediate.
5. **Delete** `Once.Internal` and the remaining bridges; `Once.Certified` instantiates `Spec.Correct`
   directly from the compiler's proof.

### 5.1 The core: the elaborator emits the Spec's core (2026-10-22, measured)

The internal core (`Internal.Core.*`) is never seen by the code generator: `MeaningBridge` relates the
checker's surface derivation to the IR directly, and `Compile` imports no core module. The core exists for
the proofs: it defines the compiler's meaning `⟦_⟧ᵈᴵ` (`Compile.agda`) and is the stepping stone to the
Spec (`SpecFront.Meaning` via `SpecCore.Sound`). Its terms (`Tm n`, `PTm m n`) have the Spec's 22
constructors in the Spec's order; the typing rules differ in five points (binder quantities in the
context, `≤qᵇ` vs `≤q`, an extra `pure ⊑π π` on fold, `sigop` by ISig membership with
`IsConcrete`/`HonestFFI` vs `(c ∶ t) ∈ Σ` at `⌜ t ⌝ᵈ`, `Respects`' form).

Route (A), chosen: the elaborator produces `Spec.Core.Syntax.Tm S m n` typed by `Spec.Core.Typing`; the
compiler's meaning (Internal domain) is re-indexed over Spec derivations at an instance `σ`; the meaning
collapse stays deferred (§5 step 2).
- Deleted (~5,150 lines): `Internal.Core.{Syntax,Typing,PolyTyping,PolyTy,PolySub,TySubst,AbsTy,Abstract,
  Schema,Derived,Rename}`, `Adequacy.{CoreAbsSem,CoreInst}`, `SpecCore.{TermR,Poly,Type,Schema,Index,
  Context,Prim}`, `SpecFront.{Abs,AbsR,Inst}`, `TypeCheck.Instance`.
- Rewritten (~38 modules, est. 3,500–4,000 lines changed): Elaboration, DerivedTyping, Core.Meaning,
  Telescope, Translate, CoreMeaningBridge, CoreEnv, CoreBridge, TeleWalk(+Invariant), ElabInst,
  ElabCommute, Laws.CoreRenameSem, the SpecCore relation (`trD = id`, `GR` becomes `≡`), SpecFront.
- New (~250 lines): the meaning of a type substitution, `⟦ty-subst-⊢ τ D⟧ σ ≡ ⟦D⟧ (τ⟨σ⟩)`, the semantic
  half of `Metatheory.TermModel.TySubst.ty-subst-⊢`.
- Biggest unknown: `sigop` — the elaborator's `View` must give each FFI name its Spec `DeclType` and
  `(c ∶ t) ∈ Σ`; the Internal meaning's `sigOpRefᵛ` recovers `IsConcrete` + ISig membership from
  `WellFormed Σ` (the classification exists in `SpecFront.SigOf`, `SpecCore.Sig`).
Route (B) (an internal core re-indexed over `Ty m`) was rejected: it rewrites nearly the same ~9,000 lines
and pays most of (A) again at the later switch.

### 5.2 Progress and what the move turned out to touch
- Types: `Internal.Type` aliases over `Spec.Type` (`rigid` deleted; `wk₀ : Type → Ty m` reads a ground
  type over `m` variables, through one named empty substitution `none`); importers take constructors from
  `Spec.Type`; `DecEq`, `Sub` (= `Spec.Sub` + lemmas), `Poly` (`substPoly` into any `Ty m`), `Instance`
  (`rigidOf T : Ty (arityOf T)`, parameter `i` = `var i`; `KindedInstance Δ` over the use's kinds),

  `Base ∅ₖ`/`WFFun ∅ₖ`; open code uses `Base Δ`/`WFFun Δ`.
- Correction found on the way: the OPEN-typed world is wider than the checker. A polymorphic body is typed,
  elaborated (`Surface.Syntax.Expr`), lowered to IR and related to its meaning (`MeaningBridge`) at its
  parameters' types; `rigid` used to make that one closed `Type`. So the checker (`Internal.Context`
  `Ctx m n`, `Scope.NamedCtx m` with a `kinds` field, `Internal.Typing`), the surface terms and their
  lowering (`⌊ var i ⌋ = Void`, as `⌊ rigid ⌋` was), and the value semantics (`⟦ var i ⟧ = ⊥`, as for
  `rigid`) are generic in `m`. Ground stays ground: imports, the signature, annotations, external operations
  (their IR built at the ground type, `⌊wk₀⌋`), events, the IR and the code generator.
- Tool: a signature generalizer (`Type` → `Ty m`, `{m}` and `{Δₖ : KCtx m}` binders, indexed contexts,
  datatypes/records, named implicits), in the session scratchpad.
- The bridges (`MeaningBridge`, `SourceFaithful`) are stated over GROUND derivations and terms only: only
  ground definitions are compiled (a polymorphic one is spliced at its instances), so nothing needs them
  over `Ty m`. A reference to a ground declaration, read at `wk₀ T`, then crosses by `wk₀-id : wk₀ {0} T ≡ T`
  and UIP (`Weaken.RelGM-wk₀`, `T∞-wk₀`, `sigop-wk₀`) — no type-by-type weakening of the relations. A
  definition's meaning is a family over any kinds (`DefFamily`), so `EnvRel` relates it at every
  `Δ : KCtx 0`.
- 2026-10-22: the types step is through everywhere outside the internal core. Keep-going pass 21: 575 modules
  check; red are `Internal.Core.PolyTy` (a private `Ty m` with `rigid`), `SpecCore.Type`, `SpecFront.Below`
  and the 92 modules above them — the core's terms carry ground `Type` annotations and polymorphic bodies
  were elaborated through `rigid`, so nothing short of route A brings them back.

### 5.5 Step 2a: the meaning collapse (route, 2026-10-23)

Today there are three meanings in the proof: the IR's (`evalᴰ`, the Kleisli domain `⟦_⟧ᴰ`, runs in
`Internal.Trace`/`Iterative` — the implementation's, it stays), the compiler's graded copy of the Spec's
(`Internal.Graded`/`GradedOps`, the surface `Denotation.Meaning` and the core `Internal.Core.Meaning`), and
the Spec's (`Spec.Domain`/`Effect`/`Core.Meaning`). `MeaningBridge` relates the IR's source semantics to
the copy (`GradedRelation`), `CoreMeaningBridge` the surface copy to the core copy (`≡`), and `SpecCore` the
copy to the Spec (`ValR`/`MR`/`T∞R`). The collapse deletes the copy: the implementation is related to the
Spec's meaning directly. The work is a closed cluster (~20 `Adequacy` modules); the per-arch proofs only
see `Compile`'s statement.
- M1 `Adequacy.SpecRelation` replaces `GradedRelation` and `SpecCore.Value`/`Monad`: a value of the Spec
