# Plan 0.116 — Effects are a Freyd category, and the meaning has one value per judgment

## 0. Why

The user's model of effects is simple: **an effect happens exactly once, in program order,
whatever the program does with the result. Usage counts copies of VALUES, never runs of
computations.** The Spec does not say this, and the gap has shown up again and again:

* **Effects vanish at quantity `Zero`** (found 2026-10-12, plan 0.112 C5 review).
  `Spec/Core/Meaning.agda` gives `⊢let` at `q = Zero` the meaning of its body alone (the
  bound computation is never run, even at `π = eff`), and `⊢app` at a `Zero` arrow never runs
  its argument. Affine `⊢sub-use` (`z≤o`) makes `let x = e in b` (`x` unused, `e` a call)
  derivable at `q = Zero` (drops the call) AND at `q = One` (makes it): **one judgment, two
  meanings.**
* **An effectful `unfold` re-runs its coalgebra TERM in every forced layer** (G3,
  `GradedOps.ana-semᵛ`): one occurrence, many runs.
* **`unfold` runs its seed before its coalgebra**; every other rule runs left to right.

Why it recurs. The rules were each right in the world they were written in: D143 (2026-09-04)
erased `Zero` arguments while "purity remains ignored", so dropping an unused computation
meant dropping a value; D250 (2026-10-01) then made purity semantic and nobody revisited the
`Zero` clauses. Meaning clauses have also been read off what the compiler does (codegen erases
`Zero` arguments, so the meaning did too). And **nothing in the Spec forces the rules to
agree**: no theorem says a judgment has one meaning whatever derivation is chosen (D305 covers
the surface's usages only), and none says what `pure` licenses. Each rule was checked alone.

## 1. The principle, categorically

A Freyd category (Power–Robinson, Levy) is the standard semantics of effects-as-arrows (D032):

* **C** — values and pure maps: `⟦A⟧ᵛ → ⟦B⟧ᵛ` (grade `pure`, `M pure X = X`);
* **K** — computations: `⟦A⟧ᵛ → T ⟦B⟧ᵛ` (grade `eff`, Kleisli(T)), PREmonoidal because `T` is
  not commutative (pairing fixes an order);
* **J : C → K** — identity on objects, `J f = returnT ∘ f` (`subM ⊑-pe`, D250), whose image is
  **central**: a pure map commutes with every computation.

What it settles:

* **Discarding.** In K, discarding a computation's result still runs the computation
  (`f ; ! = f ; (λ_ → ret tt)`). Only CENTRAL (pure) computations may be skipped. So a `Zero`
  position **evaluates and discards**; erasure is a LEMMA for pure computations (it justifies
  D143's codegen), not the definition.
* **Copying and moving.** `let-β` (substituting a computation for a variable) holds exactly for
  central `u` — which is why the term model states it for pure `u` only.
* **pure = central.** For the free monad `T` over call/halt, the centre of Kleisli(T) is exactly
  the image of J (a `call` commutes with nothing but `ret`). D250's definition and the
  categorical one coincide; that is a theorem to state, not a convention.

## 2. The guard: what makes this class of bug a type error

Two metatheorems in `Spec.Metatheory`, on the apex (`Once.Certified.language`):

* **Coherence** — the meaning is a function of the JUDGMENT: any two derivations of
  `Γ ⊢[ Ψ ] t ∷ A ! π` have the same meaning. Any rule that drops, repeats or reorders an effect
  depending on a choice the judgment hides (`let`'s `q`, sub-usage, subeffecting) makes it
  unprovable.
* **Freyd** — C, K, J as above, J a functor, J's image central, and central ⇒ in J's image.
  Any meaning clause that skips or duplicates an effectful premise breaks centrality.

Plus the rule for writing the Spec (to go into MERGE.md's Spec section / the 0.114 rules): **a
meaning clause may omit or repeat a premise's meaning only if that premise is pure**; an
effectful premise is bound exactly once with `bindM`, in left-to-right order.

## 3. Phases (strict top-down: the claim first, then the definition, then follow the red)

A false statement is never postulated (memory: don't postulate what is false), so the claim and
the definition change that makes it true land together; their proofs are scaffolded as
postulates only once they are TRUE, then discharged. Each step: change, gate (`make certified`,
backstop, merge gates), commit.

### A. The guard, stated (top)
* A1. `Spec.Metatheory.Coherence`: `∀ (d d′ : Γ ⊢[ Ψ ] t ∷ A ! π) → ⟦ d ⟧ ≗ ⟦ d′ ⟧` (pointwise in
  the target format, the interpretation and the environment).
* A2. `Spec.Metatheory.Freyd` over the graded meaning: C, K, J; J preserves identity and
  composition; `Central f` (commutes with every K-map in both premonoidal orders); J's image is
  central; central ⇒ in J's image (for the free `T`); the erasure lemma (a skipped pure
  computation is the same as one run and discarded — what D143's codegen relies on).

### B. The definition, made to satisfy it
* B1. `⊢let` at `q = Zero`: bind the RHS, discard the value. `⊢app` at a `Zero` arrow: bind the
  argument, discard it, call the erased function (`vf tt`).
* B2. `⊢unfold`: coalgebra before seed (left to right, as `app`/`pair`/`fold`).
* B3. Comments: `Typing.agda`'s "D068: … identity meaning" (`⊢sub-eff` means `subM`, D250);
  `⊢let`'s "(at `Zero` it is not evaluated — D143)"; D143's status note.
* A + B land together, A's proofs postulated (now true) in `Once.Metatheory.*`.

### C. Follow the red

  bridges), and the compiler where it erases a `Zero` argument (does the surface ever produce an

### D. Discharge the guard

* D2. Freyd: J's image central by construction; the converse by the free structure of `T`; the
