# Plan 0.116 — Effects are a Freyd category, and the meaning has one value per judgment

## 0. Why

The user's model of effects is simple: **an effect happens exactly once, in program order,
whatever the program does with the result. Usage counts copies of VALUES, never runs of
computations.** The Spec does not say this, and the gap has shown up again and again:

* **Effects vanish at quantity `Zero`** (found 2026-10-12, plan 0.112 C5 review).
  `Spec/Core/Meaning.agda` gives `⊢let` at `q = Zero` the meaning of its body alone (the
  bound computation is never run, even at `π = eff`), and `⊢app` at a `Zero` arrow never runs
  its argument. Affine `⊢sub-use` (`z≤o`) makes `let x = e in b` (`x` unused, `e` a call)
  derivable at `q = Zero` (drops the call) AND at `q = One` (makes it): **one judgment, two
  meanings.**
* **An effectful `unfold` re-runs its coalgebra TERM in every forced layer** (G3,
  `GradedOps.ana-semᵛ`): one occurrence, many runs.
* **`unfold` runs its seed before its coalgebra**; every other rule runs left to right.

Why it recurs. The rules were each right in the world they were written in: D143 (2026-09-04)
erased `Zero` arguments while "purity remains ignored", so dropping an unused computation
meant dropping a value; D250 (2026-10-01) then made purity semantic and nobody revisited the
`Zero` clauses. Meaning clauses have also been read off what the compiler does (codegen erases
`Zero` arguments, so the meaning did too). And **nothing in the Spec forces the rules to
agree**: no theorem says a judgment has one meaning whatever derivation is chosen (D305 covers
the surface's usages only), and none says what `pure` licenses. Each rule was checked alone.

## 1. The principle, categorically

A Freyd category (Power–Robinson, Levy) is the standard semantics of effects-as-arrows (D032):

* **C** — values and pure maps: `⟦A⟧ᵛ → ⟦B⟧ᵛ` (grade `pure`, `M pure X = X`);
* **K** — computations: `⟦A⟧ᵛ → T ⟦B⟧ᵛ` (grade `eff`, Kleisli(T)), PREmonoidal because `T` is
  not commutative (pairing fixes an order);
* **J : C → K** — identity on objects, `J f = returnT ∘ f` (`subM ⊑-pe`, D250), whose image is
  **central**: a pure map commutes with every computation.

What it settles:

* **Discarding.** In K, discarding a computation's result still runs the computation
  (`f ; ! = f ; (λ_ → ret tt)`). Only CENTRAL (pure) computations may be skipped. So a `Zero`
  position **evaluates and discards**; erasure is a LEMMA for pure computations (it justifies
  D143's codegen), not the definition.
* **Copying and moving.** `let-β` (substituting a computation for a variable) holds exactly for
  central `u` — which is why the term model states it for pure `u` only.
* **pure = central.** For the free monad `T` over call/halt, the centre of Kleisli(T) is exactly
  the image of J (a `call` commutes with nothing but `ret`). D250's definition and the
  categorical one coincide; that is a theorem to state, not a convention.

## 2. The guard: what makes this class of bug a type error

Two metatheorems in `Spec.Metatheory`, on the apex (`Once.Certified.language`):

* **Coherence** — the meaning is a function of the JUDGMENT: any two derivations of
  `Γ ⊢[ Ψ ] t ∷ A ! π` have the same meaning. Any rule that drops, repeats or reorders an effect
  depending on a choice the judgment hides (`let`'s `q`, sub-usage, subeffecting) makes it
  unprovable.
* **Freyd** — C, K, J as above, J a functor, J's image central, and central ⇒ in J's image.
  Any meaning clause that skips or duplicates an effectful premise breaks centrality.

Plus the rule for writing the Spec (to go into MERGE.md's Spec section / the 0.114 rules): **a
meaning clause may omit or repeat a premise's meaning only if that premise is pure**; an
effectful premise is bound exactly once with `bindM`, in left-to-right order.

### B. The definition, made to satisfy it
* B1. **Done differently, forced by the definition (D308):** "evaluate and discard" is not
  available — an erased position has no resources at run time (`Env Γ Ψ` carries nothing for a

  compile-time computation must be pure: `⊢let`'s RHS and `⊢app`'s argument are typed at
  `positionGrade q π` (`pure` at `Zero`, `π` otherwise, `Spec.Type.Grade`); the meaning is
  unchanged. Freyd's `discard-pure` is the matching theorem: only a pure computation may be
  skipped. `⊢at-position` places a pure derivation at any `q` (the surface elaborates pure).
* B2. `⊢unfold`: coalgebra before seed — moved to E: the coalgebra's computation is not bound

* B3. Comments: `Typing.agda`'s "D068: … identity meaning" (`⊢sub-eff` means `subM`, D250);
  `⊢let`'s "(at `Zero` it is not evaluated — D143)"; D143's status note.

### D. Discharge the guard
* D0 (found 2026-10-12): coherence is NOT provable at `≡`. `unfold c s : ν F eff` with a pure `c`
  has a derivation via `sub-ν` (`embν (anaᵖ …)`) and one via `sub-arr` (`anaᵈ …`): bisimilar,
  distinct coinductive values, and D201 removed bisim ⇒ ≡. Resolution (user, as for SigOp traces):
  state it up to OBSERVATION — every finite observation agrees (`projTrace ι m n` style; ν
  observed to depth `n`), an observational equivalence `≈ᵛ` defined in the Spec by type. This is
  also plan 0.112 F's equality (2) and C4′.
* D0 done: `Spec.Equivalence` (`Obs`/`ObsM` to depth `n`, `Same`/`SameM` = every depth; an arrow
  takes arguments that are `Same`); `Spec.Core.Coherence` restated: `SameM π A (⟦ d ⟧ x) (⟦ d′ ⟧ x)`
  for definitions and environments whose values are each the same as themselves (`SameDefs`,
  `Same _ x x` — the logical relation's premise; nothing in Agda makes an arbitrary function
  respect sameness).
* D1 proof architecture (`Once/Metatheory/Coherence/*`):
  1. **Why a heterogeneous relation.** The core is Curry-style: an argument's type in `app`, a
     scrutinee's in `case`, `let`'s bound type, `fst`/`snd`'s other component, `fold`'s functor and
     `unfold`'s seed are not in the judgment, and `lam`/`inl`/`inr`/`absurd` take any type. Two
     derivations of one judgment can type a subterm at DIFFERENT types (e.g. `unfold c s` at
     `ν(K (Int+Int))` and at `ν(K Int ⊕ K Int)`; a `lam` ignoring its argument at `Int ⇒ _` and
     `Float ⇒ _`). So the relation `H A A′` relates values of two types.
  2. **`H`, by recursion on the two types:** arrows logically (four quantity combinations; results
     as trees via `toT`, related leaf-wise); products/sums structurally; first-order leaves (base
     values, `μ` values, `ν` streams to every depth) through an untyped OBSERVATION encoding
     (`OTree`: int/float/unit/pair/inl/inr, plus call/halt nodes for effectful layers), compared
     pointwise — so `K (Int+Int)` and `K Int ⊕ K Int` agree; mismatched heads are `⊥` (no common
     values), `Void`/`rigid` `⊤` (none at all).
  3. **Environments with partners.** `EnvRel` relates two environments at two usages: every
     variable live on either side has a related partner on the other (the actual value if live
     there, else one that EXISTS). Needed because an erased position on one side is evaluated on
     the other (`app` at a `Zero` arrow vs at `One`): the evaluated side's subterm is related to
     the pure derivation run on the partner environment, so it is `ret` (no effect).
  4. **Fundamental lemma:** two derivations of one term `t` (any contexts, usages, types, grades)
     in related environments give `TreeRel (H A A′)` on `toT`. Non-syntax-directed rules peel on
     either side: `⊢sub-eff` is invisible after `toT` (`toT π′ (subM g m) ≡ toT π m`),
     `⊢sub-use` restricts (EnvRel is monotone), `⊢coerce` carries its types in the term (`<:`
     derivations are unique).
  5. **Close:** `H A A ⇔ Same A` (by type; `∀ n` commutes with `TreeEq` by tree induction; `μ`
     encodings injective), and `Same _ x x` gives `EnvRel` with the identity partners.
* D1. Coherence. The non-syntax-directed rules are the work: `⊢sub-use` (meaning invariant under
  restricting the environment along `⊑ᵘ`), `⊢sub-eff` (`subM` is a monad morphism — plan 0.112

  `let`/`case`/`lam` (after B1 the value is discarded uniformly; a `Zero`-bound variable is never
  read). Then the surface: its meaning is the elaborated core term's, so surface coherence is a
  corollary with the elaborator's determinism.
* D2. Freyd: J's image central by construction; the converse by the free structure of `T`; the
  erasure lemma; G7 (product β/η for central arms only) stated with it.
