# Plan 0.44 — The grand theorem IS effectful-SigOp-trace preservation (definition-first, top-down)

> **One sentence.** Fix the apex observable to be the *effectful*-SigOp trace
> indexed by **event count**, commit *only* that definition so the build breaks,
> then drive the repair strictly top-down — reconnecting the pieces we already
> have — so that "correct" finally *means* what it must mean and we can never
> again slip into middle-out / bottom-up proof.

## THE RULE (read this before touching anything)

**A principled top-level definition is correct by fiat. When the top-level and
the lower levels (existing lemmas, `what we have`, machine code) disagree, the
*top-level wins* — the mismatch is a work-item, never a reason to weaken the
top.** We do not "adjust the spec to fit the types we already have." We do not
reconstruct, bottom-up, machinery the top made unnecessary. If a lemma cannot be
connected, that is information about the *lemma*, not the spec.

This rule exists because we have repeatedly slipped: the spec drifted to fit the
cata leaf (`IRObsCorrectFlat`-as-island), and — most recently — a step-fuel
"enough-fuel" bridge was reconstructed bottom-up to fake an observable the top
had refused to represent. Both are the same disease. The cure is: **define the
top from first principles, then follow the typechecker *down*, only ever
*discharging* obligations, never *negotiating the spec upward*.**

## Why this plan exists (the mistake we are correcting)

Correctness for Once was *redefined* to be trace-shaped already — `obs`
(Plan 0.24), the `IRObsCorrect`/`MachineRefinesObs` encoding (Plan 0.36). But:

1. **Wrong altitude.** The redefinition landed at the cata leaf
   (`IRObsCorrectFlat`, an island consumed only by cata helpers) while the
   **apex** — `Once.Verified.Compile.correct`, via `compile-correct-flat` —
   stayed **value-shaped** (`ValidAtWF` headline, `StraightIR` gate) and the
   **observable stayed `Behavior = Maybe ℕ`** (an exit code). We kept building
   `value-realized` *under* an apex that still meant the old, wrong thing.

2. **Wrong index.** The 2026-06-13 draft of this plan made `Behavior` a
   *step*-indexed family ("events within `n` steps"). That conflated two
   distinct roles (see next section). It made the literal apex
   `∀ n, exec n ≡ ⟦src⟧ n` **not a true statement** — machine steps and source
   steps are incommensurate, so the same `n` does not pick out the same events on
   the two sides. D058 corrects the index to **effectful-event count**.

This revision fixes the altitude **and** the index: change the definition at the
top, first, alone — to the *event-indexed effectful trace*.

## What correctness IS (from first principles, not from the machinery)

A Once program is `main : Eff Unit Unit`. **It returns nothing.** The only thing
observable is the ordered sequence of *effectful* SigOp invocations it makes —
name + arguments, in order. There is no value channel and no final-state channel.

- **Only EFFECTFUL SigOps are observable.** `linux.exit`, `linux.write`, … are
  observable; **pure** SigOps (e.g. `arith.add.int`, emitted by the arith
  optimizer) are *not* — they are an internal optimization and contribute `[]` to
  the trace. This is grounded in `main : Eff Unit Unit`: the effect structure is
  the observable, the pure computation only *resolves* it. (`EffectShape`:
  `Pure → []`, `Emits/Halts → one event`.)
- **Programs do not produce an "exit code."** `linux.exit N` is just an effectful
  SigOp; `N` is the *argument* of that call. "Exit code" is Linux's
  interpretation of that argument — outside the compiler's concern. So **no
  `exitCodeOf`, no `Maybe ℕ`** in the spec.
- **Values / states are internal.** `ValidAtWF`, `CompiledCorr`, register and heap
  contents are never observable, so they can never *be* correctness — only
  *devices* used to prove the trace matches. `ValidAtWF` survives solely as the
  internal "the value the next effectful SigOp reads is right" lemma.
- **Full generality from line one.** Effectful algebras, arbitrary functors, and
  **non-terminating programs (server loops, `Ana`)** are all in scope. A value
  theorem *cannot even state* correctness of a server loop (no final state).

> **Correctness = the compiled program, run on the real CPU, makes exactly the
> effectful-SigOp-call sequence the source denotes — agreeing on the first `n`
> effectful events, for every `n`.**

## The index: EFFECTFUL-EVENT COUNT — and why (the part the old draft got wrong)

The trace of a total+productive program is, in general, infinite. We must
represent it without coinduction (see "no co-data" below). So `Behavior` is a
**family of finite prefixes**: `Behavior = ℕ → List SigOpEvent`. The only question
is *what `n` counts*. There are two distinct roles that the old draft fused:

| Role | What it is | Where it lives | May it be steps? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Totality device** | the finite cutoff that makes each prefix *computable* | **internal** to an interpreter | yes — internal step-fuel is fine |
| **Observable index** | what the apex `∀ n` quantifies over | the **`Behavior` type / apex** | **no — must be EVENTS** |

**The observable index must be effectful-event count.** The apex compares two
systems — the source semantics and the real CPU — whose step clocks are
*incommensurate* (the machine takes many steps per source construct, and differs
per architecture). For `∀ n, exec arch bytes n ≡ ⟦src⟧ n` to be a *true and
meaningful* theorem, `n` must name something **both sides agree on**. The only
shared currency is **the effectful events themselves**: both sides agree what
"the k-th `linux.exit`" is; neither agrees what "the k-th step" is. So
`Behavior n = the first n effectful SigOp events, in order`.

**This is the standard verified-compiler choice.** CompCert indexes program
*behavior* by the trace of observable events and lets the **simulation diagram**
(plus/star — our `flat-sim`'s `∀ fuel` induction, the injectivity-not-lockstep
principle) absorb the step-granularity mismatch. Behavior is *never* indexed by
execution steps, precisely because steps are not preserved by compilation. The
2026-06-13 "within `n` steps" draft was the deviation; event-count is the norm.

### Why still NOT a coinductive `Colist` (Plan 0.24, still binding)

A coinductive trace needs a *productive monadic bind* to sequence effects
(`tₐ >>= t_b` = concatenate sub-traces); that is **not definable under plain
`--guardedness`**, and would force `--sized-types` (rejected — has bitten this
project before). The finite-prefix family `ℕ → List SigOpEvent` keeps everything
on ordinary induction on `n` (the take-lemma): no co-data, no productive bind, no
`--sized-types`, no funext. **Event-count honors this constraint exactly as
step-count did** — it is still a `ℕ`-indexed family of finite lists. We changed
*what `n` counts*, not *that it is a finite-prefix family*. So D058 is fully
compatible with Plan 0.24's binding decision; it only repairs the index role.

### The residual fuel is a D057 concern, NOT a steps-vs-events concern

Event-count does not make fuel vanish. "Run until `n` effectful events fire" is
total only if the *pure work between events* terminates. For structured recursion
(`Cata`) it does, structurally. For the **untyped-λ `RawExpr` interpreter**
(`SS.eval`, D057's deliberately-untyped source reference) it does **not** — that
needs an internal step-fuel. That internal fuel is the *totality device* (row 1
above), it is legitimate, and it must **never leak onto the observable index**.
Whether even that internal fuel survives is a **D057** question (untyped-λ
reference vs. a structural source) — explicitly *out of scope here* and not a
reason to compromise the event-count index. Do not rebuild an "enough-fuel"
bridge on the observable; if `SS.eval` needs fuel, that fuel stays private to
`SS.eval` and the source trace is the `take n` of its emitted events.

## The redefinition (the only thing Phase 1 commits)

In `Once.Verified.Behavior` (and the immediate `⟦_⟧` / `exec` retypes):

```agda
-- WAS:  Behavior = Maybe ℕ            -- the exit code (a value — wrong frame)
-- NOW:  the EFFECTFUL-SigOp trace, EVENT-INDEXED — the first n effectful events.
Behavior : Set
Behavior = ℕ → List SigOpEvent          -- Behavior n = first n EFFECTFUL events
```

- `⟦_⟧ : Source → Behavior` — the source's intended effectful trace
  (`λ n → take n (effectful events of obs/SS.eval main)`). The interpreter's own
  totality fuel (if any) is internal; the index `n` counts emitted effectful
  events.
- `exec : Arch → List Byte → Behavior` (`ArchSemantics.exec-bytes`) — the target's
  emitted effectful trace as a function of **event depth** `n`.
- `Once.Verified.Compile.correct`, stated **pointwise in `n`** (avoids funext):
  ```agda
  correct : ∀ (arch) (src) (bytes) → compile arch src ≡ just bytes
          → ∀ (n : ℕ) → exec arch bytes n ≡ ⟦ src ⟧ n
  ```
  i.e. for every effectful-event depth, the target's and source's first-`n`
  effectful SigOp events agree (name, args, order).
- **Delete** `Maybe ℕ` / `exitCodeOf` / any exit-code projection from the spec.

Nothing else changes in this commit. The build breaks. **That is the point** —
the breakage is the typechecker handing us the exact, honest work-list, and
committing it alone makes backsliding into middle-out impossible.

## What the redefinition EXPOSES (the work-list, discovered top-down)

Forced by the spec, not chosen — each surfaces as a typecheck failure once
`Behavior` changes. **Each is a thing to discharge, never a reason to retreat.**

1. **The CPU model must PRODUCE an event-indexed trace.** Today
   `ArchSemantics.exec-bytes` returns `Maybe ℕ`, and the x86 `syscall` / `call-sym`
   is *modeled as halt* — it emits no event stream and stops at the first SigOp.
   The spec demands the target semantics *record the effectful SigOp invocation
   and continue*, exposing the first `n` effectful events (Plan 0.24's
   `instr-trace-mark` / emit-and-continue, now demanded top-down). Pure SigOps
   emit nothing. This is the deepest forced change.
2. **`StraightIR` deletion** on `compile-correct-flat` — loops/catas/`Ana` are in
   the apex; `flat-sim` is already a general `∀ fuel` simulation, so it is the
   *device* that reconciles step-granularity into event-agreement, not a blocker.
3. **Two internal bridges, composed to discharge the apex:**
   - *source ↔ flat* — the abstract machine's first-`n` effectful events equal the
     source's (`flat-events (exec-flat …)` filtered to effectful ≡ `⟦src⟧ n`).
     Already the shape of `MachineRefinesObs.traces-agree`.
   - *flat ↔ target* — the real CPU's effectful-SigOp trace equals the flat
     machine's, ridden across by `flat-sim`/`CompiledCorr` (markers are
     state-identity ⇒ trace preserved; plus/star simulation absorbs step
     granularity so the *event* prefixes coincide).
4. **`value-realized` / `ValidAtWF` demoted** from conclusion to internal device.
5. **`MachineRefinesObsF`'s `enough-fuel` / `run-halts` fields are removed.** They
   are the bottom-up *completion* artifact (they assume the run *finishes*). Under
   event-count + productivity there is no completion: the first `n` events fire
   after finitely much work and that is all the spec needs. (If a sub-lemma still
   wants the run to halt, that is a lemma mismatch — fix the lemma, per THE RULE.)

## Goal & phases (strictly top-down; no middle-out, no bottom-up)

The goal is **to close the connection** — connect the pieces that already exist so
the redefined apex is provable for the fragment we *already* claimed "proven
correct" (straight-line / Layer-0), then **link cata** into the same apex. It is
**not** to discharge cata's deep internals (paused Plan 0.36 work, resumed only
*after* the connection is proven to close).

- **Phase 0 — Reconcile the docs to the index (this revision).** `Behavior.agda`,
  D058, and this plan all say *effectful-event count*; Plan 0.24's no-co-data
  rationale is preserved verbatim; the steps-vs-events conflation is recorded as
  resolved. (Done in this rewrite + the D058 correction.)

- **Phase 1 — Break the build (the commit the discipline rests on).** Commit
  *only* the `Behavior`/`⟦_⟧`/`exec` redefinition to event-indexed effectful
  trace + the `correct` retype + deletion of `Maybe ℕ`/`exitCodeOf`. Nothing
  else. Build is red. The set of red modules = the authoritative work-list.

- **Phase 2 — Reconnect what we already "have," top-down.** Follow the typechecker
  *down*, using only existing pieces (`obs`/`SS.eval` effectful events,
  `flat-events`, `exec-flat`, `flat-sim`/`CompiledCorr`, the value-shaped
  `compile-correct` demoted to a device) to re-establish event-indexed
  trace-preservation for the **straight-line / Layer-0 fragment** we cover.
  Where a genuinely-new device is forced (the trace-producing CPU model, item 1;
  the removal of the completion fields, item 5), introduce/excise it as a *named*
  obligation — never a catchall, never a parallel structure. **If an existing
  lemma's shape (e.g. a step-indexed or completion-assuming one) does not fit the
  event-indexed top, the lemma is restated to fit the top — the top does not bend
  to the lemma.** Green for the fragment ⇒ the architecture is proven to close.

- **Phase 3 — Link cata.** Route `Cata`/`Ana` through the same apex: the existing
  `cata-correct` (`IRObsCorrectFlat`, currently an island) becomes the named hole
  *under the redefined top*, with `traces-agree`/`value-realized` plugging into the
  source↔flat bridge. Only here does paused 0.36 cata work become safe to resume —
  against a definition that will not move under it again.

## Discipline (the anti-middle-out / anti-bottom-up contract)

- **THE RULE (restated):** principled top wins; lower-level mismatch is a
  work-item. Never weaken the spec to fit a lemma; never rebuild bottom-up what
  the top made unnecessary (no step-fuel / enough-fuel / monotonicity bridge on
  the *observable*).
- **Definition first, alone.** Phase 1 commits the spec change and nothing else.
- **Follow the typechecker DOWN.** Red modules dictate order; we do not pre-build
  devices on a hunch (the error that produced `IRObsCorrectFlat`-as-island).
- **Separate the two fuel roles.** Internal totality fuel (private to an
  interpreter, a D057 matter) ≠ the observable index (always effectful events).
- **No parallel structures.** One apex, one effectful-trace observable; every
  module is consumed by the theorem above it; obligations are named/visible.
- **Value is internal-only.** `ValidAtWF` may appear as a device; never a
  conclusion of any correctness statement.
- **Full generality.** Event-indexed effectful traces, effectful algebras,
  non-terminating programs — defined once, at the top, never narrowed-then-widened.

## Non-goals

- Discharging `cata-correct`'s internals (`value-realized` machine-state
  induction, `build-layer` validity) — paused (Plan 0.36), resumed post-Phase-3.
- The effectful-cata point-free extraction (`extract-morph-eff`) — Plan 0.43.
- The source-reference representation (untyped-λ `SS.eval` + its internal fuel vs.
  a structural source) — **D057 territory**, taken on its own terms; not a
  steps-vs-events question and not settled here.
- A full coinductive trace semantics — the event-indexed finite-prefix family is
  the chosen, `--safe`-compatible representation (Plan 0.24 no-co-data rationale,
  D058 index).
