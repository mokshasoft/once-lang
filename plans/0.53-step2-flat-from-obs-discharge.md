## The one real proof: `ir-flat-correct` from `ir-obs-correct`

`ir-obs-correct ir : IRObsCorrectF ir` gives, under entry preconditions, a
`MachineRefinesObsF` whose `traces-agree`:

```
∀ k → ∃ f. take k (flat-events f (ir-to-trace ir) (mkFlat s alloc 0))
          ≡ take k (projTrace (evalᴰ ir (inject x)) k)
```

Steps to `flat-trace mir n ≡ ⟦ mir ⟧IR n`:
1. **`nothing` case**: `flat-trace nothing n = [] = ⟦ nothing ⟧IR n` (library, definitional).
2. **`just ir` case**: instantiate the entry preconditions of `IRObsCorrectF`:
   - `x = tt` (Unit input), `input-loc`/`s`/`alloc` = the loader entry frame,
     `next-slot alloc ≡ 0`, `ValidAtWF`, `BeforeFrontier`, `halted ≡ false`,
     `readReg Input1 ≡ SV-Ptr input-loc`. These are the `_start`/entry contract —
     factor as a small `entry-setup` lemma (the "entry plumbing").
3. **fuel reconciliation**: `flat-trace` uses a fixed depth `n`; `traces-agree`
   gives `∃ f`. Because a compiled `main` is total+productive, `flat-events` is
   monotone in fuel and stabilises; reconcile `∃ f` with the observable depth `n`
   via the `take`-prefix (the observable is `take n`, not raw fuel). Likely reuse
   `exec-flat-keeps-next-slot` / the `enough-fuel` witness already in
   `IRObsCorrectFlatness`.
4. **obs identification**: `⟦ mir ⟧IR n ≡ take n (projTrace (evalᴰ ir (inject tt)) n)`
   (unfold `⟦_⟧IR` — DenotTrace's event-prefix). Then `traces-agree` at `k = n`
   closes it.

Risk: steps 2–3 are the substance (entry contract + fuel↔depth). Everything else
is definitional. This is the "last gap" the x86-64 header comment already flags as
provable.
