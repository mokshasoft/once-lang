# Plan 0.42 — Discharge `gd-complete` (the `⊢ᵍ` completeness proof)

## Goal

Make Plan 0.41's value-lift **postulate-free**. Replace the TRANSIENT scaffold

```agda
postulate
  gd-complete : ∀ {ctx e A} (X : Type) → ctx ⊢ᵍ e ∶ A
              → ∃[ eE ] ∃[ d ] ∃[ f' ]
                  checkElab ctx e (X ⇒[ mk-kind Many pure ] A) ≡ success zeroUsage eE d f'
```

(in `Once/TypeCheck/Completeness.agda`) with a real proof by induction on the
`⊢ᵍ` derivation. `gd-complete` is the completeness obligation for the
`t-value-lift` bridge (`check-complete (t-value-lift gd) = gd-complete X gd`).

It is **TRUE by construction** — `⊢ᵍ` is the extractable closed-value family, so
`checkG` always succeeds on it and the value-lift `checkElabV` clauses fire — but
proving it surfaces one design wrinkle (the Unit leaf) that must be fixed first.

## Why it's true (the structure)

The elaborator routes each `⊢ᵍ`-value shape at a pure arrow to `checkG`
(`Once/TypeCheck/Elaborate.agda`), which recurses on the value and returns
`IR X A × (ctx ⊢ᵍ e ∶ A)`. So completeness is:

1. **`checkG-just`** (the load-bearing lemma): `checkG` succeeds on any `⊢ᵍ`
   value.
   ```agda
   checkG-just : ∀ {ctx e A} (X : Type) (gd : ctx ⊢ᵍ e ∶ A)
               → ∃[ m ] ∃[ gd' ] checkG ctx X e A ≡ just (m , gd')
   ```
   By induction on `gd`. Leaves (`g-int`/`g-unit`) reduce directly (`refl`);
   structural cases (`g-pair`/`g-inl`/`g-inr`/`g-In`) `rewrite` the recursive
   `checkG-just` equations so `checkG`'s `with checkG …` reduces to `just`.
   The `g-In` case also `rewrite`s the `wellFormedF? F ≡ just wfF` premise.

2. **`gd-complete`**: by induction on `gd`, show `checkElab e (X⇒[pure]A)` reduces
   to `success`. `g-int` is the direct `RInt` clause (`refl`). The structural
   cases reduce the value-lift `checkElabV` clause's `with checkG …` via
   `checkG-just` (`rewrite`), then `refl`. `g-inl`/`g-inr`/`g-In` go through the
   `ahv-*` dispatch (`classifyAppHead` reduces definitionally for the concrete
   head); `g-pair`/`g-int` are direct check clauses.

## The Unit-leaf wrinkle (fix FIRST)

`RUnit` is **not** parser-reachable (no grammar/Convert produces it), so the Unit
leaf must be a *name* (`RVar "unit"` or `RVar "terminal"`). Both currently break:

- `RVar "unit"` as a *specific* `checkElabV` clause **blocks the general `RVar`
  reduction** for abstract `x` (the catch-all-preserves-reductions trap — breaks
  the bridge lemmas), so it can't have its own value-lift clause.
- `g-terminal : ∀{ctx} → ctx ⊢ᵍ RVar "terminal" ∶ Unit` is **over-general**: a
  `ctx` with a *local* `terminal` makes `checkElab terminal` the local var, not
  the builtin, so `gd-complete (g-terminal)` is false for that `ctx`.

**Fix:** give the Unit leaf the same **lookup premises** the bare builtins carry
(cf. `t-terminal-check`):

```agda
g-terminal : ∀ {ctx}
           → lookupLocal ctx "terminal" ≡ nothing
           → lookupImport (NamedCtx.imports ctx) "terminal" ≡ nothing
           → ctx ⊢ᵍ RVar "terminal" ∶ Unit
```

`checkG`'s `RVar "terminal"` clause then `with`-checks both lookups and only
produces `g-terminal localN importN` when they're `nothing` (else `nothing` —
not a closed value, correctly). `gd-complete (g-terminal localN importN)` routes
through the **existing** `checkElab-fallback-RVar-terminal {ctx} Unit localN
importN`, and `checkG-just` reduces by `rewrite`ing the two lookup equations.
No reduction-blocking (no special-cased `RVar` clause), no shadowing.

## Steps

1. **Unit leaf fix.** Add the lookup premises to `g-terminal` (Judgment); update
   `checkG`'s `RVar "terminal"` clause to check the lookups (Elaborate). Keep the
   eff-cata test's nil as `In (inl terminal)`.
2. **`checkG-just`** in Completeness — induction on `⊢ᵍ`, `rewrite` recursive
   results (top-down: state it, let coverage/`rewrite` guide).
3. **`gd-complete`** in Completeness — induction on `⊢ᵍ`, using `checkG-just` +
   the clause/dispatch reductions; the `g-terminal` case via
   `checkElab-fallback-RVar-terminal`.
4. **Remove** the `gd-complete` postulate; `check-complete (t-value-lift gd) =
   gd-complete X gd` now references the real proof.
5. Typecheck `Completeness.agda` (green, postulate-free for this obligation);
   rebuild `malonzo` + `cabal`; re-run the exit-code suite + `compose exit@S
   (arr 7)` (still exit 7).

## Discipline

- Top-down / typechecker-guided: state `checkG-just`/`gd-complete` signatures,
  let coverage + `rewrite` failures drive the cases (the pattern that has been
  paying off — completeness as the worklist).
- Watch `with`-abstractions: `checkG`'s `with checkG …` + the `g-In`
  `with … in eqWF` are the fiddly spots; reduce them via `rewrite` of the
  recursive equations rather than re-matching.
