# Plan 0.76 — Context-Indexed Composition (D127)

**Decision:** D127. **Status:** plan (2026-08-29), not started.
**Premise:** a `compose`/`case`/`pair`/`curry` arm is an ordinary term of arrow
type in the ambient context, and the value→morphism lift is WRITTEN.

    compose emit@E 5                 -- was legal, becomes illegal
    compose emit@E (\_ -> 5)         -- how it is written
    \x -> compose emit@E (\_ -> x)   -- becomes legal; this is the point

---

## 0. What this deletes, and what it owes

**Deletes.** `⊢ᵍ` as a lifting device and its whole form-enumeration
(`g-int`, `g-float`, `g-neg-int`, `g-neg-float`, `g-pair`, `g-inl`, `g-inr`,
`g-In`); `t-value-lift`; `m-const`; `checkG`/`gd-complete`/`const-morph-strong`;
the target-driven literal dispatch (`isRIntVliftTarget?`, `isRFloatVliftTarget?`,
`checkElabV-RInt-aux`, `checkElabV-RFloat-aux`, `NegOperandView`); **all of
D126** (`t-closed-lift`, `ClosedLiftShape`, `closed-lift-aux`,
`embedOrSubsume-closed`, `checkElab-closed-lift`, `closed-lift-complete`) and,
unless the new elaboration needs it, `Once.Denotation.ThinSound`.

**Owes.** Two obligations that D127 records and this plan must actually answer,
NOT inherit as assumptions:

- **O1 — the fast path.** Closed arms must still emit `IR.∘`. As a PROVED
  equation, discharged once; explicitly not a general optimizer pass (D039
  found that unsound). Without O1 every `compose` allocates a closure.
- **O2 — what `⊢ᵐ` was forcing.** D063's realm exists so `realize-morph` is
  total and the categorical laws are forced through the agreement bridge.
  Retiring the realm as a TYPING distinction does not retire that. Phase D
  answers where the laws get forced instead. **If O2 has no answer, stop and
  re-open D127** — that is the honest failure condition for this plan.

## 1. Order of work: top-down, follow the red

The house rule (`feedback_top_down_change_first`): make the judgment change
first and let the typechecker enumerate the ripple. Do NOT pre-build lemmas.

### Phase A — the judgment (`Once.TypeCheck.Judgment`)

A1. `m-compose`/`m-case`/`m-pair`/`m-curry`/`m-cata` premises change from
    `⊢ᵐ` to `⊢ᶜ` at the corresponding arrow types. The `⊢ᵐ` data type is
    retired; the point-free leaves (`m-id`, `m-fst`, …, `m-named`) become the
    ordinary typing of the generators/imports they already are.
A2. Delete `t-value-lift`, `t-closed-lift`, `⊢ᵍ`.
A3. `composeMid` SURVIVES. Making `compose` an ordinary polymorphic constant
    (so `compose f g` is plain application and `B` falls out of unification) is
    the tempting further simplification and is OUT OF SCOPE: D044/D045 chose a
    "locally decidable, no-unification bidirectional" rule deliberately.
    Revisit only against D072's oracle, as its own decision.

**Exit:** `Judgment.agda` typechecks; everything downstream is red. Expect the
red list to be roughly: `Realize`, `Meaning`, `MeaningBridge`, `RealizeAgrees`,
`Completeness` (`morph-elab`/`StrongElab`/`morph-complete`), `Elaborate`,
`CanonPreserveMutual`, `CanonReflectMutual`, `CanonPolyTransport`,
`CanonComposeMid`, `ErrorProofs`.

### Phase B — elaboration (`Once.TypeCheck.Elaborate`)

B1. `checkCompose`/`checkCase`/`checkPair`/`checkCurry` check each arm with
    `checkElabV` at its arrow type and stop calling `extract-morph-eff` /
    `extractMorphWitness`. Those two die with the realm.
B2. Emit the exponential form
    `curry (apply ∘ ⟨ f ∘ π₁ , apply ∘ ⟨ g ∘ π₁ , π₂ ⟩ ⟩)`.
B3. Delete the literal dispatch (A2's surface half): a literal has ONE meaning
    at one type, and `checkElab` stops being target-directed at literals.

**Exit:** the elaborator typechecks and `\x -> compose emit@E (\_ -> x)`
elaborates. Correctness is not yet claimed.

### Phase C — O1, the closed-arm specialization

C1. State it: for closed `f`, `g`, the Phase-B composite denotes `f ∘ g`.
    One equation over the IR denotation.
C2. Prove it once (β/η at the exponential; `SourceFaithful.faithful` is the
    existing lever and is postulate-free).
C3. Use it in codegen ONLY — closedness becomes a codegen-side decision with a
    proof obligation attached, never a typing-side premise. This is the whole
    point of D127: the representation property stays a representation property.

**Exit:** closed composes emit `IR.∘` as before; `exit-tests` on all three
arches unchanged.

### Phase D — O2, and the meaning

D1. `realize`: one clause set over `⊢ᶜ`; `realize-morph` folds in.
D2. `⟦_⟧ᵐ` folds into `⟦_⟧ᶜ`.
D3. `MeaningBridge`/`RealizeAgrees`: `bridge-m` and the morphism agreement
    clauses fold in. **`StrongElab` and `morph-elab` disappear** — with them
    the syntactic `m ≡ realize-morph mᵐ` obligation that blocked D126's
    morphism half, and the layering inversion behind it (`RealizeAgrees`
    imports `Completeness`, so the fact D126 needed sat above the layer that
    needed it).
D4. **Answer O2 explicitly, in writing, before closing the phase.** Candidate:
    the laws are forced by the agreement bridge over `⊢ᶜ` directly, since the
    IR side is unchanged and `faithful` already relates `elaborate` to the
    denotation. If that does not hold, say so and stop.

### Phase E — migration and gate

E1. Five surface sites use a literal as a compose arm
    (`neg-int-value-lift.once`, `closed-expr-lift.once`, `memory-stress.once`,
    `threads.once`, and `compose 17 emitLit` in the value-lift test). Rewrite
    to `\_ -> …`. Retire `closed-expr-lift.once` (it tests D126).
E2. Add the test D127 is FOR: an arm capturing an enclosing binder.
E3. Full gate: judgment sweep, 7 gate roots, island backstop, MAlonzo
    re-extraction + cabal sync, exit tests ×3 arches, `cabal test`.

## 2. Risks, in the order they can kill the plan

1. **O2 unanswered** — the laws lose their forcing. Failure condition; re-open
   D127 rather than paper over it.
2. **O1 not provable as stated** — then every compose allocates, and the
   performance/representation story regresses. Measure before believing it is
   fine; do not assume β/η is free.
3. **`cata`'s algebra widens.** It is a `⊢ᵐ` morphism today; under F it becomes
   a Γ-indexed term, which admits a CAPTURING algebra — a real semantic
   widening. Decide it deliberately (its own decision entry) rather than
   inheriting it from this refactor.
4. **Blast radius.** `⊢ᵐ` is woven through eleven modules (§Phase A exit).
   This is a redesign, not a patch.
