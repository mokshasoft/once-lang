# Plan 0.91 — Program facts belong in the obligation

## 0. The one measurement

Two of the three postulates in `Once.Adequacy.ArchCorrectness.FlatFromObs` are
FALSE, and `entry-witness` feeds both into `ir-obs-correct`. So the apex chain

    once-certified → once-compiler → arch-correctness → <arch>-correct
                   → ir-flat-correct-of → ir-obs-correct

is proved against ⊥-derivable assumptions. Progress is ONE number: how many of
`{block-runs, entry-size}` are gone. `rewrite-preserves-of` is out of scope —
D213 established it is a different, honest class.

**The compiler is not wrong.** 746 tests pass on three arches; the emitter mints
labels correctly and `find-thunk` finds them. Nothing here should change
behaviour. This plan makes the proof say what the compiler already does.

## 1. THE RULE FOR THIS PLAN

**Top-down only.** Every step below changes a CONSUMER — a statement someone
else depends on — and then follows the typechecker to whatever that breaks.
No step builds a lemma because the pieces for it exist.

This is not a style preference. This area is already littered with the
alternative: `link-block-split`, `link-pre`, `link-post` (SMCore),
`FlatSteps-middle` (FlatStepLemmas), `curry-bl-in`/`curry-bl-agree`
(LabelScope) are all built, correct, and reachable from NOTHING, because they
were written for a consumer nobody wrote. `FlatStepLemmas.agda:359` even
documents the intended architecture. D212 deleted three more of the same.

**Consequence, and it is the point:** those six lemmas are NOT the plan. They
are hypotheses about what the plan will need. A step may use them only when a
stated goal demands them. If the goals turn out not to demand them, they are
deleted, not retrofitted.

## 2. THE DIAGNOSIS (D213, restated as the design)

The emitter returns two channels:

    ir-to-trace' : … → ℕ × ℕ × AbstractTrace × List (LabelId × ℕ × AbstractTrace)
                                    ↑ trace         ↑ block table

`IRObsCorrectF` states a placement premise for the trace (`SpanAt`) and NONE
for the block table. A closure's meaning is not a function of the state alone —
memory holds a NAME; the binding from name to code lives in the program. So the
missing premise is the block table's placement, and `block-runs` was an
assumption standing in for it.

`entry-size` is separate and simpler: `program-bound` is universally quantified
inside the module while `ir-size` is unbounded. It must be chosen per-program.

## 3. STEPS

Each step names the CONSUMER it changes and the obligation that change creates.
Nothing is built before the typechecker asks for it.

### S0 — Anchor: change the STATEMENT, never blank the body

**CORRECTED 2026-09-16, after the first attempt used the wrong instrument.**

Holing the body (`entry-size ir = {!!}`) leaves the TYPE unchanged. Agda then
emits NO INTERFACE for the module, so every consumer fails to LOAD rather than
to typecheck: the full-root run reports exactly one error — an `import` line in
`X86-32.agda:97` — and the rest of the cone is simply unbuildable. That is not
top-down. It destroys information instead of producing it.

**The rule, and it is the heart of this plan:**

> Top-down means changing a TYPE, never blanking a BODY. A hole REMOVES
> information; a changed statement PRODUCES it.

Going red at every consumer is not a cost of this method — it is the entire
benefit, and it only happens when the STATEMENT moves. So S0 is: state the
obligation the way it should have been stated, keep the module elaborating, and
let each consumer that can no longer supply its premises fail AT ITS OWN CALL
SITE. That list of failures is the work-list, and it is free.

*Creates:* the true list of who depends on these, and how — as type errors,
not as a dead build.

*Evidence this works:* S1's two successful designs never used S0's work-list.
They changed the STATEMENT (a bound-indexed family) and followed the resulting
ripple. S0-as-first-written contributed only a grep-derived consumer chain.

### S1 — `entry-size`: move the quantifier (independent of S2+)  ✅ DONE (D214)
**Landed 2026-09-16.** Outcome was stronger than planned: the bound was not
moved, it was DELETED. `program-bound` never had a reader — every use only
weakened it to feed a sub-IH — so the premise came out of `IRObsCorrectF`
itself and the whole telescope went with it (26 modules, apex to leaf, zero
new postulates). `ClosureWellFormed` now carries its own `{body-bound : ℕ}`.

The consumer is `entry-witness`, and above it the arch modules that instantiate
`program-bound`. Change the apex so the bound is chosen AFTER the IR — e.g.
`ArchCorrectness` takes `ir` and instantiates at `suc (ir-size ir)`, or
`program-bound` becomes an existential the apex discharges.
*Creates:* whatever re-threading the arch modules need. Expect the three
per-arch modules and `ArchCorrectness.agda` to move.
*Do this FIRST:* it is separable, it is the smaller of the two, and it
validates the top-down loop on this area before the harder one.

### S2 — `IRObsCorrectF` gains the block-table placement premise
Add, beside `SpanAt`:

    BlocksAt prog (bodies-of (ir-to-trace' n l ir))

with `BlocksAt prog bs` = every `(ℓ , b , t) ∈ bs` is bound in `prog`:
`find-thunk prog ℓ` resolves to where `t` begins.
*Creates:* every one of the eight clause parts must now THREAD it (the
D204/D206/D208/D210 ripple, fifth time), and every caller must SUPPLY it.
*Note:* the exact shape of `BlocksAt` is a guess until S3 reports what the
consumers actually demand. Write the weakest thing that makes S3's goals
statable, and expect to revise it.

### S3 — `curry` / `Ana` consume it; `apply` / `Out` stop assuming
`obs-correct-curry` and `obs-correct-Ana` mint blocks — they now have
`BlocksAt` in scope and must produce whatever records their own block's
binding. `obs-correct-apply` and `obs-correct-Out` currently take the
resolution from `BlockRuns`; they take it from the witness instead.
*Creates:* the real statement of what a closure witness must carry. THIS is
where the shape of the fix is decided — not in S2.

### S4 — `block-runs` narrows or disappears
With S3 done, re-examine what remains of `BlockRuns`. Either it is fully
discharged, or what survives is the genuine reachability invariant ("the
closure this state holds was built by an earlier `curry` in this run"), stated
over runs rather than over arbitrary states.
*Creates:* the residual axiom, named precisely, with an emptiness probe run
against it before it is accepted.

### S5 — the apex proves `BlocksAt (ir-to-trace ir)`
`entry-witness` must now supply it for the whole program. This is the
induction over `ir-to-trace'` that D188's comment called "provable" and nobody
wrote.
*Creates:* the first real demand for D168's stranded machinery — IF the goals
demand it. Check, do not assume.

## 5. WHAT NOT TO DO

Do not start at S5 because the machinery is sitting there. That is how this
area got into its current state.

Do not tighten `SV-Code`'s payload to make labels unforgeable. It closes the
probe without fixing the cause — a program fact bolted onto a memory word —
and it is the wrong level: relocation is a property of how a unit is laid out,
not of what a code cell may hold.
