# Plan 0.89 — Relocatable compilation units

**Decision:** D159. **Branch:** `plan-0.89-relocatable-units`.
**Status:** roadmap (2026-09-07).

The compiler shall output RELOCATABLE code, and that shall be encoded in the
emitter's codomain (OCP-0005 style: the violation is unsayable) rather than
asserted as a premise. See D159 for the argument and D157/D158 for what forced
it.

**Method, and it is not negotiable:** top-down. Make the top-level change, let
the typechecker produce the obligations, and fix what goes red. Do NOT build
supporting lemmas in advance — D154 is the standing lesson: a lemma whose shape
was derived by reasoning about a proof you have not written is a guess.

---

## 0. Where we are

`comp-value-realized` is proved (D158) but through `SpanAt prog base` — a
threaded placement premise, which is the weak encoding. `obs-correct-curry` and
`obs-correct-apply` remain axioms because `curry` inlines its body into its own
trace, so no single placement maps both it and `apply` (D157).

`IRObsCorrectFlat`: 17 named residuals. Apex green on
`plan-0.83-0.80-0.59-spec-independence` at `4f1e1bdb`.

**The channel already exists at three levels and nothing fills it** —
`ir-to-trace'`'s fourth component, `ir-to-bodies{,-from}`, and
`emit-thunk-body` in all three backends. Phase A is mostly REVIVAL.

---

## Phase A — the encoding (the type change)

The whole point. Everything else is consequence.

* **A1.** `CompUnit` in `Once.CCC.Codegen.IRToTrace`:
  `entry : AbstractTrace` (block-local positions only) and
  `blocks : List (LabelId × ℕ × AbstractTrace)` — the shape the fourth
  component and `emit-thunk-body` already use: (label, frame budget, trace).
* **A2.** `ir-to-unit : IR A B → CompUnit`, `link : CompUnit → AbstractTrace`,
  and `ir-to-trace ir = link (ir-to-unit ir)` so the APEX STATEMENT IS
  UNCHANGED and the arch pipeline keeps consuming a linked image.
* **A3.** `curry` emits its body as a BLOCK instead of inlining it. This deletes
  `c-jmp (ℓ o end-label)` and `end-label` — they exist only to jump over the
  inlined body — halving `curry`'s label usage.
* **A4.** `Cata` likewise: its algebra becomes a block. Its own comment already
  says the algebra "runs in its OWN frame as a called body (exactly as `curry`'s
  body is generated)"; today `cata-dispatch` splices it.
* **A5.** Composition of units is UNION of block maps. State it; do not yet
  prove anything about it.

**Exit:** `ir-to-trace` unchanged extensionally where it matters; the emitted
asm gains real `.L_thunk_` blocks (the backends' dead path comes alive).

---

## Phase B — the machine runs units

* **B1.** `fpc` becomes `LabelId × ℕ` (block, offset within block). `fret` and
  `flink` likewise — they hold return SITES.
* **B2.** `find-label` becomes BLOCK-LOCAL; `find-thunk` becomes block lookup.
  "A jump never leaves its body" becomes true by construction.
* **B3.** `Shifted` and the twelve `shifted-*` lemmas leave the composition
  proof. They are not deleted — see Phase D.

**Watch:** ~924 `fpc` sites, 26 files. The three arch correspondences are
insulated by Phase D1, not touched here.

---

## Phase C — the obs layer sheds its placement arithmetic

* **C1.** `SpanAt prog base` / `k + base` / `shuffle` / `length-++` (D158) are
  replaced by block-map inclusion `u ⊆ prog`, which composition preserves
  definitionally because composition is union.
* **C2.** Re-prove `comp-value-realized`. It should get SHORTER than D158's — no
  offset arithmetic, no `handover-eq` shuffle.
* **C3.** `traces-agree` reindexed AND chain-bounded (the D158 staged item): an
  `∃ fuel` event prefix over-collects the successor's events once a fragment
  runs inside a larger program. `chain-events` (FlatEvents) already exists.

---

## Phase D — link correctness (where relocation goes to live)

* **D1.** `link-correct` — `link` preserves behaviour. `Shifted` /
  `exec-flat-reloc` are exactly this proof, since `link` places blocks at
  offsets. ONE global lemma instead of a per-composition side condition.
* **D2.** Unit well-formedness: block labels distinct. This is `LabelScope`'s
  real content, stated once on the unit. NOTE: `LabelScope`'s `SegAgree`
  induction is measured-but-unlanded; check whether the unit form needs it at
  all before investing.
* **D3.** The three arch correspondences keep consuming the linked image, so
  `pc-off`/`ret-eq`/`blk-off` are unchanged in this plan.

---

## Phase E — closures, which is what all of this was for

* **E1.** `valid-closure-wf` carries the body's LIVE witness (a `ValueRealized`
  over the body's block, ∀-placement), replacing `BodyCorrect`.
  Structural note: `IRResultAWF` and `ValidAtWF` are mutual ONLY because
  `valid-closure-wf` carries `BodyCorrect`; dropping that field breaks the
  cycle, so the island vocabulary can move next to the nine `*WF` modules
  instead of being deleted.
* **E2.** `obs-correct-curry` produces it (from the body's IH);
  `obs-correct-apply` consumes it via block lookup. These are the two remaining
  exponential axioms.
* **E3.** `apply ∘ curry body` — the D157 counterexample — closes because the
  union contains the body's block.

---

## Phase F — optional, later

Collapse `blk-off` (412 sites) into `caddr + within-block offset`. The symbol
table already exists concretely (`caddr : LabelId → ℕ`, built by
`code-map cprog`), and `code-eq` exists only to reconcile it with `blk-off`.
Under Phase B that reconciliation becomes definitional. Do NOT start this until
Phases A–E have shown how much it actually buys.

---

## Sequencing note

Phase E is the goal; Phase A is what makes it possible. Do not start E against
the D158 offset-shaped interface — it would be re-threaded afterwards.
