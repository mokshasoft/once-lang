# Plan 0.159 — Optimisations are directed refinements: the same behaviour, never more cost

## Summary

The optimizer rewrites programs into faster ones. Today Once proves that every rewrite keeps the
program's behaviour, but nothing says that a rewrite is an IMPROVEMENT, or by what measure: plan
0.129 found a rewrite that made binaries grow only by measuring after the fact. This plan gives Once
one relation, "`u` refines `t`": the two behave the same, and `u` costs no more than `t` by a chosen
measure (run time, memory, code size). Unlike "the same", it has a direction: it composes (two
improvements make an improvement), it holds inside any larger program, and it cannot be used
backwards. Every optimizer rule becomes a proved improvement for the measures it serves, the
compiler can choose rewrites by measure, and later a library can ship its own optimisation rules
with their proofs.

## 1. Why

- **Optimizer correctness is stated without direction.** D347: each pass keeps the trace
  (`Adequacy.OptimizePreserves`), a symmetric fact. Why a rule is applied left to right, and what it
  buys, lives in comments and in measurements (plan 0.129 C2: dropped because binaries grew).
- **Measures conflict.** Inlining and specialisation save run time and grow code (C2). A single
  "better" cannot be defined; a per-measure one can, and is what a cost-directed pass selection needs.
- **Plan 0.147 §5 left "laws as directed paths" to evaluate.** Its candidate reading
  `Hom t u = t ⟶* u` (reduction) does not fit optimisation: reduction counts β-steps of one strategy,
  says nothing of allocations or code size, and some good rewrites are not reductions (fusion,
  `⟨fst, snd⟩ → id`).
- **The DT kernel already has the structure.** Its `Hom` is transitive, congruent (`ap`: a term's action
  on a path) and provably NOT symmetric (`SpikeNoSym`: `sym` is false at the universe). That is the
  algebra of an improvement relation. On master it is needed as a relation in the Spec, proved in Agda,
  with no dependent types.

## 2. Where we are (read 2026-11-27)

- **The optimizer** (`formal/Once/Optimize.agda`): ten passes of `optimize-once` over the IR: type-
  directed rules (a quiet map into `Unit` is `terminal`, out of `Void` is `initial`), identity,
  associativity, the product and sum β-rules (also through a chain), closure β, η up to `Unit`/`Void`,
  dead code. A rule that drops a term does so only when it has no effect (`has-effect?`).
- **Its theorem** (D347, `Once.Adequacy.OptimizePreserves`): a logical relation; the optimized program
  has the original's trace.
- **Sameness** (`formal/Once/Spec/Equivalence.agda`): two computations are the same when no world tells
  them apart (weak bisimilarity of traces, values related by sameness); defined once per type former
  (`RelPair`, `RelSum`, `RelFun`, `RelMu`, `RelStream`, `RelM`). Symmetric by construction.
- **Cost.** `Once.Spec` is about meaning only (plan 0.147 §3.7.1); cost belongs to the implementation
  Spec, beside `NoLeak` and the layout invariant. Static cost: `once stats`,
  `tests/codegen-measure.sh` (MERGE.md §1). Dynamic cost: plan 0.130 (not started) and `LinCore`'s
  event trace (plan 0.146, which also decorates runs with alloc/free pairs, A13).
- **D468 (proposed)** retires the IR: L1 passes run over the Spec's graded `Tm`. The relation should be
  stated there, so it survives that move.

## 3. The change, three ways

### 3.1 Through a category theorist's eyes

- **A locally ordered category.** Types are objects, terms are morphisms, and each hom-set carries a
  preorder `t ≼ u` ("`u` refines `t`"). Composition is monotone in both arguments. That makes the
  core a category enriched in preorders (a locally posetal 2-category up to the equivalence of
  sameness): the 2-cells are improvements.
- **Precongruence.** Monotone composition and monotone formers (pairing, case, λ, fold) say that an
  improvement of a subterm is an improvement of every term containing it. That is the DT kernel's `ap`.
- **No symmetry.** `≼` implies sameness (both directions of behaviour), but not `u ≼ t`. That is the
  DT kernel's missing `sym`, and the reason the relation is not an equivalence.
- **Improvement theory.** This is Sands' improvement theory (Moran–Sands, for call-by-need), here over
  Once's own semantics: an improvement is a contextual refinement that never costs more, and its
  standard tools (an improvement-preserving context lemma, "improvement induction") apply.
- **Indexed by a measure.** One preorder per cost measure `c`, `≼[c]`, and the product order for several.
  A measure is a monotone map from runs (or emitted code) into an ordered monoid, compatible with
  sequencing.

### 3.2 For the programmer

- **Nothing to write** for ordinary programs: the relation is the compiler's.
- **What is gained.** The compiler's promise becomes precise: `once build` never makes a program slower
  (or larger, or more allocating) by the measures it was asked to respect. A flag such as an
  optimisation profile picks the measures.
- **Later (D4): certified rules in libraries.** A library may state an optimisation law as an
  improvement (`map f . map g ≼ map (f . g)`, a fold fused with its producer), proved once, which the
  optimizer may use at every call site: GHC's `RULES`, but proved, oriented, and with their cost
  stated. This is plan 0.147 §3.5's "laws the optimizer may use", made directional.

### 3.3 At run time

- **No cost of its own.** The relation is a theorem about rewrites. It adds nothing to emitted code.
- **What it prevents.** A rewrite that regresses a requested measure is no longer applied: C2-style
  regressions are excluded by proof for the measures in force, not found by measuring.
- **What it enables.** Cost-directed rewriting: among rules that are improvements for the requested
  measure, apply them, with the guarantee that the composite is one too (transitivity).

## 4. Decisions

- **D1. Which measures.** Candidates: (a) dynamic events (calls, rounds of a loop: the trace length);
  (b) heap allocations (alloc events, plan 0.146 A13); (c) code size (emitted words, `once stats`);
  (d) stack depth. Recommended: start with (a) and (b), which are properties of a run and compose
  along sequencing; (c) is a property of the code, not the run, and needs its own compatibility proof
  (inlining shows it is not monotone for every rule). Open (the user).
- **D2. Where it lives.** Sameness is in the Spec (meaning). A measure is implementation Spec (plan 0.147
  §3.7.1). Recommended: `≼[c]` defined in the implementation Spec as "same in the Spec's sense AND no
  more cost by `c`", so the Spec itself stays about meaning only. Open (the user).
- **D3. The reading of `Hom`.** Plan 0.147 §5's `Hom t u = t ⟶* u` versus improvement. Recommended:
  improvement on master (§1's reasons). The DT kernel's `Hom` stays a type in the logic; whether its
  extraction relates to `≼` is a DT-side question (`POC/DirectedHoTT/PLAN-GRADED.md`), not needed here.
  Open (the user).
- **D4. User-visible rules** (§3.2): in this plan, or after plan 0.147's structures. Recommended: after,
  since a rule needs a declared structure to be stated over. Open (the user).

## 5. Steps

1. **Spike (no Spec change).** On the CCC fragment of `Once.Optimize`'s rules, define `≼[c]` for
   measures (a) and (b) over the existing trace semantics, and classify each of the ten rule families:
   improvement for (a)? for (b)? neutral? For (c), measure with `tests/codegen-measure.sh` which rules
   grow code. Record the table in §2. This step answers whether the relation is the right one before
   anything depends on it.
2. **The relation** (user approval for D1–D2): `≼[c]` in the implementation Spec; reflexive,
   transitive, implies sameness; monotone in every core former (the precongruence lemma, the
   analogue of `ap`).
3. **Restate the optimizer's theorem.** `OptimizePreserves` becomes "each pass is a `≼[c]` for the
   measures it claims", per rule family from step 1. A pass that is an improvement for none of the
   requested measures is not run under that profile.
4. **Over the graded `Tm`** once D468 lands: the same statement for L1 passes, so the relation outlives
   the IR.
5. **Cost-directed profiles.** The CLI's optimisation profile names its measures; the pass pipeline is
   filtered by step 3's table. Measure before and after (plan 0.130, plan 0.146's perf comparison).
6. **Certified library rules** (D4, after plan 0.147 §3.5): a rule is an improvement law over a declared
   structure, checked at declaration, used by the optimizer.

## 6. Done when

- `≼[c]` exists for the chosen measures, is a precongruence, and implies sameness.
- Every optimizer rule is proved an improvement for the measures it is used under, and no pass runs
  under a profile whose measures it does not improve.
- Plan 0.147 §5's "laws as directed paths" is settled (D3).
