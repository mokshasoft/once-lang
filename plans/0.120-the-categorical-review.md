## Findings

**A. Definitions that do not mean what they should**
1. Freyd claims in the wrong category: `Commute`/`Central` use bisimilarity, which sees block boundaries;
   meanings are observational, so a silent step then `return y` is observationally `return y` yet "not
   central". State them up to observational sameness.
2. `EqM act` is `≡` on trees with continuations: its laws need function extensionality (`Once.Postulates`,
   used by `Metatheory.Effects`, `Lawful`, `Recursion`). One equality for every grade: bisimilarity of the
   image in `T∞`.
3. `Once.Spec`'s header still says "free (completely iterative) monads".

**B. Non-canonical constructions, duplicates**
4. `T∞ X` is `νM act (K X ⊕ Id)`: two definitions of one final coalgebra, two unfolds
   (`loop`/`loopBlock`, `anaA`/`treeA`/`mapA`). Define `T∞` as the stream and `loop` as its unfold.
5. The stream unfold has no uniqueness claim (finality); `loop-unique` should be its instance.
6. `Grade.EffGrade` and `Signature.Effectful` are one predicate.
7. An operation at `eff` adds nothing: one call is the generic effect in `T`. Operations are `act`; `eff`
   by subsumption (`sigopᵛ`'s `eff` clauses, `dt-eff`, `e-eff`, `EffGrade` go).
8. Halting: a `void` answer is a nullary operation; the world gives a dummy `⊤` (`Answer void`) and
   `resume` splits on all six base types. Observation halts on an empty answer type; the world answers
   only inhabited ones.

**C. Missing laws, vacuity**
9. The kernel property (sameness respects `>>=`) and sameness as a PER are proved, not claimed.
10. No claim that a well-formed signature has an Interpretation: without it sameness at arrows is vacuous.
11. Conversions compose: `conv refl` is the identity and `conv (trans p q)` the composite (up to `Same`).

**D. Larger**
12. Behaviour is the final coalgebra: a trace `νY. Delay (End + Event × Y)`, sameness its weak
    bisimilarity; the depth family and its prefix laws become derived. Reshapes `Correct` and D4.
13. `ν F eff` has no introduction (only the coercion from `ν F act`): drop it (amends D317 item 3).
14. `Interp` mixes the model of the pure declarations (read by the meaning) with the world's answers (read
    by observation): split.
15. `BTy`/`⟦_⟧ᵇ` beside `Type.Base` (with `toB`/`fromB`): one notion of first-order type.

**E. Minor**
16. `composeᶜ` binds `g` at `Many` (comment: linear in each arm; `pairᶜ`/`caseᶜ` use `One`).
17. `EffCod` uniformly: `unit`, `void`, or neither empty nor a singleton.
18. `intLits`: a helper for the `RNeg` cases.
19. `layerM`, `νpa`/`νpe`/`νae`, `anaP`/`anaA`: instances of one unfold (an Agda guardedness workaround),
    justified by 5's universal property.

## POCs (evidence: `plans/0.120-poc/`, checked `--safe` in a scratch worktree)

- **A — `T∞` as a stream** (`ResumptionAsStream.agda`). With `ν F eff` allowed, one mutual block of `M` and
  `νM` fails positivity (an `eff` stream's layer type puts the stream inside a functor code). Without it
  (item 13), no mutual block is needed: streams with `pure`/`act` layers, then `T∞ X := νM act (K X ⊕ Id)`,
  then `M`. Bind and the unfold are guarded; `loop := anaA (K B ⊕ Id)` with its computation law by `refl`.
  One generic stream bisimilarity (`Bisim`, through the layer), `T∞`'s `≈` its instance. **Items 4 and 13
  go together.**
- **B — behaviour as a trace** (`Traces.agda`, `TraceOf.agda`). `Tr X` with `next : Delay (End X ⊎ (Event ×
  Tr X))`; weak bisimilarity `WB` from arrivals both ways (as `Delay`'s `≈⟨_⟩`). Proved, guarded:
  `fromDepth` (agreement at every depth ⇒ `WB`: what today's compiler proofs give, so D4 can reach it),
  `toDepth` (the converse), `WB-trans` (corecursive). The trace of a `T∞` computation against a world
  (`trace`, a call per visible step, a block's end a silent step) is guarded when every step is a copattern
  clause. **Item 12 is feasible**; `Run`/`Related` (D1) become the finite-prefix lemmas.
