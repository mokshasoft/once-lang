  The 17 constructors that have existentials take 58 of the 89 s.
- **Cause:**
  - Each `okT_k` (the typing of tail level k at the constructor's values)
    was a k-deep weakening tower. Summed over the levels, that is cubic in
    the existentials. The towers also mixed `w3 j` with
    `w1 (renTm vs (w1 j))`, which conversion then has to compare.
  - `dPv` is a chain of `⊢-cast`s over `T⁽k+1⁾-sub`/`-cong`, with
    `wk-cancel-tm` once per argument. That is quadratic.

## Fix 1 (written, unchecked): per-level typing lemmas

In the row generator, after `okinst`, `gen-judge.py` emits one lemma per
level, at variables:

```agda
okT⁽k⁾ : {Ξ} {xs es} → (typings of the positions the remaining codes cite) → TelOK Ξ J (T⁽k⁾ xs es)
```

- The top level (k = n) is the body instance.
- Level k < n is `okσ (code k) (okT⁽k+1⁾ {_} {w1 x…} {var vz} weakened-typings here-typing)`.
  The weakening is `wkN`/`wkQ`/`wkT`/`wkG`/`wkK`, one per position, done once.
- Only the positions that the remaining codes cite (`cited(k)`) carry a
  typing. A `Fin J` hypothesis is allowed but never weakened, and an assert
  guards that.
- The row typing is now `okT⁽0⁾` at the sources. In `gen_con`, `okt(k)` is
  one application of `okT⁽k⁾` at the values, with no tower.

**Red:** a profile of the old and the new Red both run out of the 4 GB heap

went from 21.3G to 15.1G, and the new lemmas add 8.7G. A sweep line of
