## Rules learned this stretch

- Knot or Lib material at a concrete, elaborated signature re-runs the
  elaborator on every read. State lemmas over an abstract `𝒮` with
  `K ⊑ᴰ 𝒮`, and instantiate them (`⊢refAt`).
- A segment's check covers only its own entries, so a family is a segment.
  Don't put several families in one segment.
- Probe caps for bisecting must sit above the import closure's own load
  (≥5 GB here), or every probe "fails".
