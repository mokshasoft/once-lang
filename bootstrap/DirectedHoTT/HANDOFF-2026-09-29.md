## 2. Measurements worth keeping

- **Context-form mismatch ⇒ opaque** (memory `context-form-mismatch-opaque`).
  A big closed code elaborated at `(⌊Ξ⌋∙)∙` and at `⌊(Ξ▹A)▹B⌋` is compared
  by normalising it (~35 s a meeting). Every such code is opaque: `sub0`,
  `wk`, `FIBMₒ`, `⌜Ty⌝`, `⌜Tm⌝`, the SubEnv operations, each family's
  `⌜·⌝` and SynPat's `CASE`. The effect: `⊢app` 80 s → 5 s,
  LookupCon 58 s → 10 s, Judge 92 s → 14 s.
- **Goal-directed typing.** Contexts flow from the goal and only terms are
  pinned: `tr`'s row went 216 s → 6.4 s.
- **Constructors by tails + `mono-by`** (memory
  `constructors-via-tails-and-mono`). 225 generated constructors
  typechecked on the FIRST run. A `⟶*` between two instances of a
  substitution-natural object is one `mono-by`, never a hand chain.
- **Memory is the binding constraint on this 7 GB box.** When another
  session's agda runs, a 4 GB module is killed (143) and retried with
  `-c`. Constructor modules are split by meaning (⟶ ξ vs computation:
  4.2 GB → 2.5/2.9 GB).
- **The import linter had three ERROR blind spots**: declarations in
  `opaque` blocks, indented or `where` imports, and record-expression
  fields. They produced 33 false errors and a sweep refusal that exits 0.
  All three are fixed (`tools/lint-imports.py`, with a live control).
- **`gen-judge.py --check`** now covers every generated module and flags
  modules it no longer writes (the deletion pass).
