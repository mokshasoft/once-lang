Lessons, each one in memory:

- **Opacity is the lever for big closed codes.** A code compared at two
  syntactic forms is normalised. The forms differ by context
  (`context-form-mismatch-opaque`) or by literal against `suc`
  (`literal-vs-suc-index-mismatch`). Making the carrier opaque fixes it.
  Changing the numerals instead moved the cost into the lemma bodies:
  Knot1 +29 %, reverted.
- **Explicit absurd clauses that split a NESTED derivation are the
  cost.** A catch-all congruence after the redex clauses suffices, since
  Agda drops constructors whose unification fails
  (`redex-views-kill-head-enumeration`).
- **Keep split-first openers.** Removing `trstk?-red-d`'s opener took it
  from 10 s to 34 s.
- **A principled abstraction that compiles slower is profiled, never
  discarded on a timing** (user, `slower-abstraction-profile-dont-discard`).
