## The results worth carrying forward

### 1. ★★ THE DEPTH-FORDED SHAPE SCALES TO FIELDS

`_▹_` is a shape the table did not have. `Var`'s depth-Forded rows carry
at most ONE ordinary field beside the Ford; `_▹_` carries **two**, and
**both sit at the BOUND field's depth, not at the ambient's**: `Ctx d →
RTy d → Ctx (suc d)` targets `suc d`, so nothing in the row rides `⟨d⟩`.
⚠ `PLAN-JUDGEMENT` step 0 got the row formula right and then priced it as
*"an ordinary cross-sort field at the ambient depth, so it costs nothing
new"* — the two halves of that sentence contradict each other, and the
second is the wrong one. Five telescope slots,
the deepest in the table after `ordtr`'s six.

★ **AND IT COSTS ONE `kCast` AND ONE `⊢-cast`, NO MORE.** Every mangled
form of the two numerals comes back by `num-ren`/`num-sub`, one rung per
action, exactly as the `Var` rows do. **The extra field lengthens the
chains without adding a KIND of obligation.** ⇒ nothing here argues
against step 1's three-component index telescope.

⚠ And `◇` costs nothing at all: it Fords the depth to `0`, so its ambient
is the CLOSED `pair sCtx nzero` and both actions COMPUTE on it —
`Knot/Terms`' situation rather than `Var-vsK`'s.

## NEXT — and the gate is now NAMED AND PRICED, not just named

### A. ⬜ OBJECT-LEVEL WEAKENING **OVER THE KNOT** — the whole gate

`PLAN-JUDGEMENT` step 1 (`_∋_∷_`) reads *"needs only the weakening
already in hand"*. ⚠ **THAT IS TRUE OF THE MECHANISM AND FALSE OF THE
CODE.** `Examples/WkTm` weakens a **3-constructor toy**; `_∋_∷_`'s index
mentions `renTy vs A`, which must be an `ielim` over the **55-row knot**
returning a knot element.

★★ **AND IT CANNOT BE 55 ENUMERATED METHODS.** That is precisely the
147s-vs-5s mistake of 2026-08-26 (`half-generalization-is-worst`), with a
SYNTAX codomain instead of `Nat`. It has to be `Lib/IFold`-shaped: method
computed from the `ICon`, tuple from the `IDesc`.

⚠⚠ **BUT `Lib/IFold`'s SHAPE DOES NOT REACH IT, AND HERE IS WHY.** A fold
into a constant motive takes the IH at every `iρ`. A weakening does not:

* `dκ`'s `RTy ε` field is pinned at the LITERAL index `pair sTy nzero`.
  Under the motive `M(i,t) = K (pair (fst ⟨i⟩) (suc (snd ⟨i⟩)))` its IH
  lands at depth `1` — but the rebuilt row needs it back at depth `0`.
  ⇒ that field must take the **original field**, not the IH.
* the four depth-Forded rows (`vz`, `vs`, `◇`, `_▹_`) constrain `snd ⟨i⟩`,
  so the shifted index needs the ford witness **re-proved at a bumped
  `m`** — `WkFin`'s `⊢jsub` transport, four times.

⇒ **the per-field choice — IH or original — is a function of whether that
field's index expression MENTIONS THE AMBIENT.** That is decidable on
`ICon`'s raw `RTm Δ` indices, so a generic `Lib/IWk` is plausible; it is
not established, and it is the fork worth spiking before building.

★ **SPIKE IT AT THREE ROWS, NOT 55.** `cTy-Nat` (ford-only), `cTm-lam`
(the binder, index `suc ⟨d⟩`), `cDCon-kap` (a LITERAL-pinned field beside
a riding one) bracket every case in the table, and `cVar-vs` adds the
transport. Done when the per-field rule types, or the obstruction is
named. Everything in `PLAN-JUDGEMENT` §3 steps 1–4 is downstream of it.
