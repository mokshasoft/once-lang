## The results worth carrying forward

### 1. ★★ THE DEPTH-FORDED SHAPE SCALES TO FIELDS

`_▹_` is a shape the table did not have. `Var`'s depth-Forded rows carry
at most ONE ordinary field beside the Ford; `_▹_` carries **two**, and
**both sit at the BOUND field's depth, not at the ambient's**: `Ctx d →
RTy d → Ctx (suc d)` targets `suc d`, so nothing in the row rides `⟨d⟩`.
⚠ `PLAN-JUDGEMENT` step 0 got the row formula right and then priced it as
*"an ordinary cross-sort field at the ambient depth, so it costs nothing
new"* — the two halves of that sentence contradict each other, and the
second is the wrong one. Five telescope slots,
the deepest in the table after `ordtr`'s six.

★ **AND IT COSTS ONE `kCast` AND ONE `⊢-cast`, NO MORE.** Every mangled
form of the two numerals comes back by `num-ren`/`num-sub`, one rung per
action, exactly as the `Var` rows do. **The extra field lengthens the
chains without adding a KIND of obligation.** ⇒ nothing here argues
against step 1's three-component index telescope.

⚠ And `◇` costs nothing at all: it Fords the depth to `0`, so its ambient
is the CLOSED `pair sCtx nzero` and both actions COMPUTE on it —
`Knot/Terms`' situation rather than `Var-vsK`'s.

## NEXT — and the gate is now NAMED AND PRICED, not just named

### A. ⬜ OBJECT-LEVEL WEAKENING **OVER THE KNOT** — the whole gate

`PLAN-JUDGEMENT` step 1 (`_∋_∷_`) reads *"needs only the weakening
already in hand"*. ⚠ **THAT IS TRUE OF THE MECHANISM AND FALSE OF THE
CODE.** `Examples/WkTm` weakens a **3-constructor toy**; `_∋_∷_`'s index
mentions `renTy vs A`, which must be an `ielim` over the **55-row knot**
returning a knot element.

★★ **AND IT CANNOT BE 55 ENUMERATED METHODS.** That is precisely the
147s-vs-5s mistake of 2026-08-26 (`half-generalization-is-worst`), with a
SYNTAX codomain instead of `Nat`. It has to be `Lib/IFold`-shaped: method
computed from the `ICon`, tuple from the `IDesc`.

⚠⚠ **BUT `Lib/IFold`'s SHAPE DOES NOT REACH IT, AND HERE IS WHY.** A fold
into a constant motive takes the IH at every `iρ`. A weakening does not:

* `dκ`'s `RTy ε` field is pinned at the LITERAL index `pair sTy nzero`.
  Under the motive `M(i,t) = K (pair (fst ⟨i⟩) (suc (snd ⟨i⟩)))` its IH
  lands at depth `1` — but the rebuilt row needs it back at depth `0`.
  ⇒ that field must take the **original field**, not the IH.
* the four depth-Forded rows (`vz`, `vs`, `◇`, `_▹_`) constrain `snd ⟨i⟩`,
  so the shifted index needs the ford witness **re-proved at a bumped
  `m`** — `WkFin`'s `⊢jsub` transport, four times.

⇒ **the per-field choice — IH or original — is a function of whether that
field's index expression MENTIONS THE AMBIENT.** That is decidable on
`ICon`'s raw `RTm Δ` indices, so a generic `Lib/IWk` is plausible; it is
not established, and it is the fork worth spiking before building.

★ **SPIKE IT AT THREE ROWS, NOT 55.** `cTy-Nat` (ford-only), `cTm-lam`
(the binder, index `suc ⟨d⟩`), `cDCon-kap` (a LITERAL-pinned field beside
a riding one) bracket every case in the table, and `cVar-vs` adds the
transport. Done when the per-field rule types, or the obstruction is
named. Everything in `PLAN-JUDGEMENT` §3 steps 1–4 is downstream of it.

### A′. ⚠ AND STEP 0 IS IN TENSION WITH STEP 2 — **AT ONE ROW, `◇`**,
###     ✅ **SPIKED AND CONFIRMED** — `Examples/Knot/WkEmp`

The row walk below was derived on paper; `◇`, the row it turns on, has
since been **built and type-checked**. See the two ✅ blocks.

The obvious motive for a knot-wide weakening is the UNIFORM shift, which
is what both `WkTm` and `WkFin` use:

    M(i,t) = K (pair (fst ⟨i⟩) (nsuc (snd ⟨i⟩)))

Walking the 55 rows against it, **54 are fine** and the costs are the ones
already known:

| row shape | what the method does | cost |
|---|---|---|
| ford-only (`cTy-Nat`) | tag ford passes through: `fst (pair (fst ⟨i⟩) …)` **βfst-reduces to the field it was given** | none |
| riding index (`cTm-lam`) | take the IH; its index is the shifted one already | `ixConv`, as everywhere |
| LITERAL-pinned field (`cDCon-kap`'s `RTy ε`) | ⚠ take the **ORIGINAL field**, not the IH — the IH lands at depth 1 and the row needs depth 0 | none, but it is the per-field rule (A) is about |
| depth-Forded (`vz`, `vs`, `_▹_`) | bump the bound `m` and re-prove `snd ⟨i⟩ ≡ suc m` under `nsuc` | one `⊢jsub`, **in 4 rows, not 55** |

★★ **AND `◇` IS THE ONE ROW THAT BREAKS.** Its method holds
`snd ⟨i⟩ ≡ nzero` and must prove `nsuc (snd ⟨i⟩) ≡ nzero` to rebuild
itself. It cannot. Weakening a CONTEXT is not a thing.

⚠⚠ **AND IT DOES NOT FAIL — IT FABRICATES.**

> ✅ **BUILT, AND IT COMPILES** (`Examples/Knot/WkEmp`, `--safe`, trust
> surface still empty). `K (sCtx, 1)` **is** inhabited, so the method can
> be written with a different constructor:
>
>     ⋄  ↦  ◇ ▹ Nat        -- a type that was never there
>
> It is well typed, and Agda cannot tell. There is no `Ctx`-weakening
> spec for it to violate. ⇒ **the hazard is a GREEN build, not a red
> one**: `verification-that-covers-less-than-it-claims`, reached from a
> new direction — and the reason the file was written rather than
> argued, as `Examples/Vec.no-cons-at-zero` was.

★ **AND IT IS DEAD CODE, WHICH IS WHY THIS IS A FORK AND NOT A BLOCKER.**
No syntax sort has a `Ctx` field — `Ctx` is mentioned only by JUDGEMENTS —
so a traversal entered at `sTy` never reduces the `◇` method. `renTy`
built over the uniform motive would be *correct where it is used* and
would merely CLAIM more than it delivers.

⇒ the fork, and it is a DESIGN call rather than a cost one
(`principledness-over-edit-cost`):

| | |
|---|---|
| **(a) split `Ctx` into its own `IMu`** | `KnotD` goes back to 53 rows and weakening is uniform and honest. `_▹_`'s `RTy` field becomes `iκ (⌜IMu⌝ KnotD IPair …)` — legal, because §12 put `⌜IMu⌝` in `U`, though a foreign-`IMu` field has never been built. Re-opens step 0. |
| **(b) keep one description** | Build `wk` at the uniform motive, write `◇`'s method as DELIBERATE junk with a header saying so, and state adequacy only at the syntax sorts — the `Examples/Vec.no-cons-at-zero` treatment. Costs nothing today and leaves a type that overclaims. |
| (c) make `Ctx` depth-degenerate | ⛔ **NO.** `◇` would inhabit `K (sCtx, 5)` and the depth would stop measuring the context — junk inhabitants that the adequacy map cannot see. |

> ✅ **AND THE SECOND RESULT — THE COST OF A PAIR-INDEXED TRANSPORT — IS
> TWO `jsub`s, FLAT.** One per index component, where `WkFin`'s `fsuc`
> moved along one. ⚠ **And the `wk-single` one expects does NOT appear.**
> The outer transport's family mentions the inner component, weakened
> past the transport's own binder and then substituted — the round trip
> that is PROPOSITIONAL in general. Here it is DEFINITIONAL, because that
> component is `snd` of a VARIABLE and both actions COMPUTE on variables.
> `Knot/Build`'s finding (c), in the place it was least expected.
>
> ⇒ **measured, after a wrong guess**: replacing either `wk-single` with
> `refl` type-checks, so both casts came out. The first draft carried
> them and would have recorded a cost that is not there.
>
> ★ ⇒ **`_∋_∷_`'s THREE-component telescope should cost three `jsub`s,
> flat** — the first quantitative thing anyone knows about step 1.

⇒ **the fork is now the only thing outstanding**, and it is decidable on
the evidence above rather than on a guess. Under (b), `WkEmp` is already
the row whose junk is written down rather than discovered; under (a) it
becomes the record of why `Ctx` was split out.
