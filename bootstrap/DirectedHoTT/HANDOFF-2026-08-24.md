## 1. THE FOUR DESIGN FLAWS, and why they are the real output

Every one was found by trying to PROVE something, never by reading the
rules.  §9.1 came from subject reduction, §9.2 from `Vec`, §10.1 from
`fund`, §10.4 from `trCS` — the LAST metatheorem in the chain.

* **§9.1** methods were typed at ONE index → obligation (c) was FALSE.
* **§9.2** `iρ f` could not express §3's own `Vec` → telescope.
* **§10.1** `iki-κ` demands a κ-interpretation at EVERY environment, and
  `Θ ⊢ κ ∷ U` does not give one.  ⇒ `ICodeWf`: a κ field is a CLOSED
  small type or a FORDING CONSTRAINT, and nothing else.
* **§10.4** `⌜IMu⌝` had NO J rule, and a closed
  `tr (⌜Hom⌝ c a m) (hrefl (⌜IMu⌝ D I i) s) e` is typeable — so PROGRESS
  was FALSE, not merely unproven.  ⇒ `tr-J-IMu`.

⚠ **The classifiers had `⌜IMu⌝` wrong THREE ways, all by catch-all**
(`stkC?`, `stkA?`, `stablecd?`).  `check-formers` check 3 lists exactly
these defaults and says "CONFIRM each rather than inherit it"; had that
been done when the former landed, §10.4 would have been a one-line row
instead of a reduction rule threaded through eleven modules.

## 3. TRAPS FOUND THIS SESSION — do not re-learn these

* **TWO MODULES WERE COMMITTED UNVERIFIED, and one did not PARSE.**
  `LogicalRelation` carried a mangled `wne` clause (`sne-elim` had lost
  its `with`; the `sne-ielim` twin beside it was malformed and had the
  wrong arity).  `Fundamental/Syntactic` was missing the entire indexed
  anti-renaming layer.  Both were inside the "GREEN and committed" table
  of the previous handoff.  ⇒ **a green is a claim about module M only
  if `Checking …M` is in the log** — the trap the previous handoff itself
  records, hit again one commit later.
* **`fst`/`snd` are TERM FORMERS, not projections.** `fst (pair a b)`
  reduces by `βfst`; it is NOT definitionally `a`.  Two attempts at a
  short `vlen-cons` died on that, and it is why the payload's
  projections cost real steps in the chain.
* **A `where`-bound definition does not unfold for the unifier** the way
  an inlined term does.  `consPay` in `Examples/Vec` had to be inlined
  before the `β` steps would match.
* **Bulk clause duplication must copy the CLAUSE, not the LINE.**
  Duplicating `⟹-⁺` rows line-wise split multi-line clauses and produced
  a parse error; and a clause whose `ptr-J-Mu` is on the RHS needs its
  LHS pattern changed instead (`p⌜Mu⌝` → `(p⌜IMu⌝ _)`), not copied.
* **A dot pattern carries the code too**: `.(⌜Mu⌝ _)` must become
  `.(⌜IMu⌝ _ _ _)` in the twin, and `{t = … (⌜Mu⌝ Dᵐ) …}` likewise.
* **Termination beats modularity.** `iκW`/`interpIK`/`interpID` were
  written in `Fundamental/Indexed` with `elW` passed in as an argument —
  clean, and it made `fund`'s termination check FAIL, because the call
  then sits under a λ with an unknown derivation.  They live in
  `Fundamental` itself; everything that is NOT mutual with `fund` stays
  in `Indexed`.
* **When a former is a FOURTH kind, ask whether it should be.** `⌜IMu⌝`
  being neither `pw?` nor `stkC?` nor ⌜Nat⌝ made `CodeFate` inexpressible
  and `UProg` want a fourth arm.  Adding the arm works until `trCS`,
  where the configuration turns out to be TYPEABLE and no step exists.
  The fourth kind was the symptom; the missing reduction rule was the
  disease.
