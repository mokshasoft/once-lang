## 2. ★ Where it stopped: the Pw POC is blocked, and the decision

The Pw POC is the migration recipe on the smallest family; the plan is in
PLAN-BIDI §3g. Pw's Hom constructor needs the Knot's `wk`, so `wk` must
become a core entry `#wk`.

- `#wk` = an `ielim` over `ref #KD`, the motive written out, the Lib's
  method tuple `TRAVM` lifted with holes. **The elaborator cannot fill
  it:** unannotated λs sit in synthesis position, and a bidirectional
  elaborator cannot infer their domain.
- ⇒ **Lifting with holes (`↑`) works for first-order DATA (`KD`, `CtxD`),
  never for Lib CODE.**
- ★ **DECIDED (user): option 1.** The Lib's generic machinery becomes
  CORE definitions: the traversal is written once over any description,
  and `wk` is its instance. User: "Kernel and MT proofs are still pretty
  simple compared to the Knot."
- **That needs one KERNEL increment**, because `Desc` has no eliminator
  (only the built-in folds `dpay`/`dih`/`ielim` consume one):
  - (a) a dependent `Desc` eliminator, or
  - (b) `Desc` itself levitated.
- Rejected, with reasons in §3g:
  - annotating from the Lib's derivations: it inlines `KD` and fails
    "code a human would write";
  - patching the elaborator per idiom: a dead end in principle.

**Lazy δ is DEFERRED until measured.** Nothing measures slow yet. When it
lands, the hard part is the VIEWS (`viewΠ`/`viewΣ` lift erased types back
and need the invariant "every ref is a signature ref"), not
`decConvFast`. §3g has the details.

## 4. Practicalities and gotchas

- **Another Claude session (the compiler, `Once/CCC/…`) runs Agda on this
  box.**
  - A sweep was OOM-reaped mid-run today. A 143 next to its process is
    contention, not a verdict.
  - A full cold sweep is best run offline:
    `rm -rf _build/2.8.0/agda/DirectedHoTT`, then
    `tools/sweep.sh > log`, then check for the `ALL GREEN` line.
- **Elaborator probes:** `why-entry n ≡ "ok"` by `refl` prints the
  elaborator's path and reason. `why wfSig` can run out of memory; probe
  one entry.
- Use `chk.sh`-style runs with `AGDA_RTS="-A64m -c"`. A bare `check.sh`
  without `-c` was killed (143) on Core today.
- **A new Knot module needs `tools/gen-trust.sh`** (else the sweep
  refuses, and exits 0).
- **Hand-written Knot rows** go through gen-judge's `HANDROWS`. A typing
  row is the rule PLUS the head's conversion row (`TCVat k`).
- A row module that the reduction family imports must not import
  `JudgeConv` (cycle). That is why `Ref` and `RefJudge` are separate.
