### ⚠⚠ THREE CORRECTIONS TO THE PLAN — and each makes the result stronger

**(1) `jsub` IS NOT ON THIS PATH. Do not re-attempt the `⌜Π⌝`-family
transport.** The route was opened as *transport first, then reduce*: move the
auxiliary's bound off the stuck `μ x` along an `Id`, so `amrec-unfold-s` can
fire. That framing is what forced the certificate to be bound INSIDE the
family and what made the transport's source obligation certificate
irrelevance. But what pieces 6–8 actually produce is **bound** irrelevance as
well — `aux x n₁ a c₁ ≡ aux y n₂ a c₂` at two INDEPENDENT bounds — so the
bound moves directly and `⊢jsub` never enters, with neither its ban on the
family mentioning the proof nor its demand that the family typecheck at an
arbitrary `v`. The 2026-08-14 obstruction analysis was correct *about
`jsub`*; the conclusion "so we need irrelevance" was right and the
intermediate step turned out to be unnecessary.

**(2) The premise is an INEQUALITY, not an identity.** The plan wanted
`Id Nat (μ x) (nsuc k)`; what is needed is `Hom Nat (μ x) (nsuc k)`, because
the auxiliary at ANY bound above the measure computes the same answer. An
identity gives the inequality; the converse is false.

**(3) `StepExt` is needed at a VARIABLE carrier.** Not stated anywhere before
and it is the whole shape of what remains — see below.

### The design decisions worth keeping

- **Index the motive by the AMBIENT RENAMING `θ`, not by a `vs`-tower.**
  Every branch of the induction lands at a different depth; with the tower
  spelled out each depth needs its own peel — that is what `wᶠ¹/²/³-single`
  are, and they ran out at three. With `θ` abstract there is exactly ONE peel
  (`peelθ`) and the branches differ only in which `θ` they pass. This is the
  single change that made piece 8 affordable.
- **The pointwise calculus** (`subren`/`subrenTy`/`renren`/`renrenTy` +
  `extcond`/`extcondR`, top level): *a substitution that meets a renaming is
  another RENAMING, and which one is decided variable-by-variable.* The
  caller supplies a two-case bridge and gets the collapse. Every peel in
  pieces 7–9 is an instance. ⭐ Reach for this before writing another
  tower lemma.
- **`irrT'` takes every slot already at the depth it is used**, so `subTy` and
  `renTy` distribute by `refl` (the `aAuxB'` trick) and all the naturality
  lives in the eight peels of `irrT-sub`/`-ren`.
- **The inner split is a CASE SPLIT, not a recursion** — the second bound
  never descends. That is why two bounds cost ~1.5× one and not twice, as
  risk 10 predicted.
- **`idOfRed` needs a mirror.** `idToRed` (Id between sources → Id between
  reducts). Both directions are needed and they are not interchangeable: a
  proof arrives at whichever end its producer left it.

### ⚠ The one place the route could have stalled — recorded because it will recur

The (S,S) leaf instantiates the IH at the recursive call, and `⊢app` wants
that call's **certificate typed**. The certificate the reduction hands over
is `descS` under SEVEN substitution layers; `subTm` does not invert, so no
amount of subject reduction produces a typing for it, and inversion would
need Π-injectivity plus uniqueness of types. The fix is `descS-peel`: say
what the certificate IS — `ordtr (nsuc μy) μa (nsuc k) q p` — and let
`⊢strong-step` type it from the two hypotheses the pointwise premise already
hands over. Five argument peels, two of them `refl` because the two variables
reach their slots by computation.

⇒ this confirms the 2026-08-15 probe: the "substitution-naturality for every
arithmetic template" item that `gcd-gt-term`'s comment defers is **not** on
this path.

## WHAT IS LEFT — gcd's `StepExt`, and it is NOT one instantiation

The 2026-08-14 handoff said a step that uses its IH once "discharges
`StepExt` by ONE instantiation of the hypothesis". **That is true only per
recursive leaf, and getting to the leaves is the work.** Here is the actual
shape, determined 2026-08-15 by reading `gcdStp`.

### Why it is not free

`StepExt` quantifies over an ARBITRARY carrier `a`, and the induction
consumes it at a **variable** — piece 8's leaves apply it at
`⊢irr-a : … ⊢ var (vs (vs vz)) ∷ renTy (θ₃ θ) A`. That is forced: the motive
`irrT` quantifies `a` internally, because the recursive call's carrier is
bound inside the pointwise hypothesis.

But gcd's step reduces only at a CONSTRUCTOR-HEADED carrier:

```agda
gcdStp  = lam (natrec G1z (natrec G2z (natrec G3z G3s (monusTm …)) (fst …)) (snd (var vz)))
gcd-gt-term : … → RecCall (app (app gcdStp (pair (nsuc a') (nsuc b'))) ih) ih …
gcd-le-term : … → RecCall (app (app gcdStp (pair (nsuc a') (nsuc b'))) ih) ih …
```

At a neutral `a` all three scrutinees — `snd a`, `fst a`, `monusTm _ _` — are
stuck, so `app (app gcdStp a) ih₁` and `app (app gcdStp a) ih₂` are two stuck
neutrals differing only inside. **There is no funext in this kernel** (`ap`
needs a `Hom`, a `flat?` code and a one-variable family — it cannot lift a
POINTWISE equality of two IHs), so they cannot be related by a congruence.

### The route that DOES work — internal case analysis on the step's own scrutinees

`natrec` proves `P(t)` for a NEUTRAL `t` perfectly well: the motive is
`λ z → P(z)` and the scrutinee is `t`. So abstract each of gcd's three
scrutinees out of the goal and split on it. Three nested `natrec`s, four
leaves:

| leaf | branch | IH used? | proof |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `snd a ⇝ 0` | `G1z` | no | both sides are the SAME term ⇒ `idrefl` |
| `fst a ⇝ 0` | `G2z` | no | same ⇒ `idrefl` |
| `monus ⇝ 0` | `G3z` | once | the pointwise hypothesis, at `G3z`'s own call |
| `monus ⇝ S` | `G3s` | once | ditto |

The two base leaves are free (`idrefl` — the terms are literally equal, no
hypothesis needed). The two recursive leaves are ONE instantiation each, as
the old handoff said — but they need the recursive call's carrier and
certificate, and:

⚠ **PREREQUISITE, unchanged from 2026-08-14:** `⊢G3z`/`⊢G3s` build their
certificate INLINE, so it has to be pulled out as a named sub-derivation
first. `descS-peel` (piece 8) is the template for how to name a certificate
that only exists under substitution — but note gcd's is *already* a named
sub-derivation once `⊢G3z`/`⊢G3s` are refactored, so this should be cheaper
than `descS-peel` was.

⚠ **THE MOTIVE ABSTRACTION IS THE COST.** Each of the three splits needs
`subTy (single scrutinee) Motive ≡ goal`, i.e. the scrutinee un-substituted
out of a goal that mentions the step's whole case tree twice (once per `ihᵢ`).
The pointwise calculus (`subren` etc.) is the tool; budget it like one leaf of
piece 8, ×3.

## Standing lessons (carried forward, plus new)

- **Agda cannot invert `subTm`.** Anything occurring only under a substitution
  must be **named**, never left as `_`. Signature of the failure: an *unsolved
  meta*, never a wrong solution. ⭐ 2026-08-15 corollary: this is also why
  subject reduction cannot type a certificate that only exists as a reduct —
  `descS-peel` is the shape of the fix.
- **Green ≠ meaningful.** For every conditional lemma, ask whether the premise
  is dischargeable *at the arguments it is stated for*, and write the witness.
  `check-formers.sh` check 6's regex is anchored at column 0, so nothing
  inside a `module` — i.e. the whole WF library — is visible to it; for
  anything under `AmT`/`AmTΠ` the vacuity question must be asked by hand.
- **Prefer the pointwise bridge to another tower lemma.** Measured this
  session: the tower form needs a new lemma per depth and the depths are
  unbounded; the pointwise form needs one lemma and a two-case bridge per use.
- **When a plan says "transport, then reduce", check whether the theorem the
  transport's obligation forces you to prove is already strong enough to skip
  the transport.** It was, twice over (bound irrelevance ⇒ no `jsub`; ≤ rather
  than `Id` ⇒ weaker premise).
