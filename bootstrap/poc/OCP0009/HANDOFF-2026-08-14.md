## Gap C — DONE

`gcd (2,1) = 1` runs through **two** recursive turns, exercising both branches:

```
(2,1)  2∸1 = 1 ≠ 0  ⇒ a>b branch ⇒ recurse at (2∸1 , 1)   -- gcd-gt-term
(1,1)  1∸1 = 0      ⇒ a≤b branch ⇒ recurse at (1 , 1∸1)   -- gcd-le-term
(1,0)  base case    ⇒ return 1
```

`gcd-2-1` and `gcd-1-1` are in `NbEPDirDBExamplesGcdLib`. Each turn is one
`aux-cycle` plus a reduction computing the recursive argument; nothing between
turns is bespoke. `gcd-2-1` checked green **first try**, which is the evidence
that the cycle is a loop and not a one-off.

### What made it possible (`NbEPDirDBLibAmrec`)

`amrec-step-s` could describe the *first* unfolding and no other. Three defects,
each fatal alone:

1. **The bound was pinned.** `amrecTm` is `aAuxTm m` — the auxiliary at bound
   `μ x` only. But `ihS`'s head is `var (vs⁴ vz)`, the `natrec`'s *own* IH
   variable, so a recursive call lands on `auxIH x k`, never back on `amrecTm`.
   ⇒ `aux-unfold-z/-s`, `aux-step-s` take the bound as a parameter.
   `amrec-step-s` is now **derived** from them — the faithfulness check.
2. **`P` was flat**, quantified outside `ih`, so it structurally could not
   mention the recursive call. ⇒ `aux-step-sF` takes `P : RTm → RTm`.
   Same bug one level down for the certificate ⇒ `q` is a family too.
3. **Index and argument were conflated.** Measured: `auxIH x k ≡ auxIH y k` is
   **not** `refl` — the measure family really does mention the carrier slot.
   After one turn the auxiliary specialised to `x` is applied to a different
   `y`. With them identified, turn 2's source can never match turn 1's target.

`aux-cycle²` composes two turns abstractly with no transport between them.

⚠ **Measured trap:** stating the loop-closer with an *existential* certificate
OOM-kills the module (exit 143 under the cgroup cap) — `with`-abstracting over a

### Why 3/4 cannot reach variables as reductions

`monusTm m n = natrec m (predTm (var vz)) n` recurses on its **second**
argument, and `⟶*` only fires on constructor-headed scrutinees. Eq 3 demands
`⟶* nsuc d`, satisfiable by `a := suc d` with `d` a variable. Eq 4 demands
`⟶* nzero`, which forces `a ⟶* zero` — and a variable never reduces, so eq 4's
witnesses are ground. This is a property of reduction, not of the formulation.

### ⚠⚠ THE NEXT DELIVERABLE, RESTATED — it is NOT `amrec-unfold-Id`

The plan below was **transport first, then reduce**: `jsub` along
`p : IdN μx (nsuc k)` to move the auxiliary's bound off the stuck `μx`, then let
`amrec-unfold-s` fire. It does not work, and the reason relocates the remaining
work a second time.

```agda
amrec-unfold-Id : Δ ⊢ p ∷ IdN μx (nsuc k) →
                  Δ ⊢ ? ∷ IdN (app amrecTm x) (app (app stp x) ih)
```

**⛔ (i) `jsub` cannot state it.** `⊢jsub` (`NbEPDirDBType.agda:599`) transports
a **code family** `d` over a variable `v : El ⌜Nat⌝`. Two consequences, and they
close the route between them:

* `d` may not mention the proof (that is J, which the kernel does not have), and
* `d` must be well-typed at `U` for an **arbitrary** `v`, not just at `μx` and
  `nsuc k`.

`d` must mention `auxIH x v` or it says nothing about the unfolding — and
`auxIH x v` only reaches an `El _` applied to a carrier **and** a certificate of
type `Hom Nat (μ of that carrier) v`. At an arbitrary `v` that type has no
inhabitant to weaken in from `Δ`. So the certificate must be **bound inside the
family** (`⌜Π⌝` over `⌜Hom⌝ ⌜Nat⌝ (w μx) (var vz)` — the only formable option),
and the transport's source obligation is then universally quantified over
certificates while the combinator ever supplies one, `reflTm μx`.

⇒ **the source obligation IS certificate irrelevance.**

**⛔ (ii) And even with it, eqs 3/4 do not follow.** Their right-hand side is a
fresh `app gcdTm Y` — the auxiliary at bound `μ Y` with certificate
`reflTm (μ Y)` — while the left-hand side's recursive call is
`app (app (auxIH X k) Y) descS`: bound `k`, index still `X`. Relating those is
bound-and-certificate irrelevance again.

⚠ **A correction to this document's own gap-A section.** The measure is *not*
what blocks eqs 3/4: `μ (suc a , suc b) ⟶* nsuc (a + suc b)` fires at
**variables** (both components are constructor-headed), so the cycle lemmas do
apply there. What fails to compute is `monusTm`'s branch selection — precisely
what `gcdGtH`/`gcdLeH` internalise. The earlier "the measure is stuck on the
neutral `a`" is true of `gcd (a , b)` at bare variables, not of eqs 3/4.

### THE REAL DELIVERABLE — irrelevance, from step EXTENSIONALITY

Irrelevance is reachable without touching the kernel, at a price the **caller**
pays once. It follows from *step extensionality* — "the step's answer depends on
its IH only pointwise" — by **internal** induction on the bound:

* bound `0` — the IH that branch hands over is `⊢strong-base'`, i.e. **ex
  falso**, so any two certificates agree for free; `Id (El C) a b` is reachable
  by `absurd`, being `El (⌜Id⌝ C a b)` up to `El-⌜Id⌝`.
* bound `suc k` — both sides reduce to `app (app stp y) ihᵢ`; the two IHs differ
  only in the certificate they pass down, and that difference is the induction
  hypothesis at `k`.

Extensionality is the **standard** side condition — Agda's own
`Induction.WellFounded.FixPoint` demands exactly it — and gcd's step can
discharge it, since it uses its IH exactly once (`RecCall`).

⇒ **the interface change**: `AmTΠ` takes an extensionality premise. Order of
work: (a) state extensionality and irrelevance as `RTy`s; (b) the induction;
(c) `amrec-unfold-Id` by the `⌜Π⌝`-family transport above; (d) index
irrelevance, which eqs 3/4 need on top and which is *not* yet analysed.

⚠ **Carried forward.** `⊢gcd-b0-Π` is not a template: it never uses its IH,
because `gcd (a,0) = a` holds by the outer `natrec`'s *zero* branch. Eqs 3/4 are
the recursive branches — their content *is* the recursive call.

**Risk: gcd's recursive-call certificate — typed by `sub-lemma`, no arithmetic

⚠ **And that probe corrected the `StepExt` interface.** The two `ih` typing

⚠ **And `check-formers.sh` check 6 will not catch that**, which is a blind spot

**Why 4 is the gate, and it was not obvious.** The pointwise half of

needs `ihᵢ`'s own type — which no lemma provides: `⊢ihZ`/`⊢ihS` type the branch

**Interface note on `StepExt`.** It is stated META-LEVEL and
context-polymorphically, not as an `RTy`: the internal Π-form would carry the

it already has. It takes **no** typing premise on `ih₁`/`ih₂` — checked against
both sides: a provider reduces `app (app stp a) ihᵢ` with `ihᵢ` opaque (which is
exactly what `gcd-le-term` already does, taking its `ih` as a bare `RTm`), and
the consumer has its own from piece 4.

⚠ **A dependency this surfaced, for gcd's eventual discharge of `StepExt`.** The

## Tools built this session (reusable)

- **`_⟫_` and `one`** (`NbEPDirDBExamplesGcdStep`) — right-associative
  `⟶*-trans`, an Elm `|>` for reductions. An 8-step derivation is 8 lines with
  **zero** nesting parens. Miscounting the old closing-paren run cost a
  debugging cycle; prefer this for every new chain.
- **The peel family** `pk2`…`pk6`, `peel4`, `peel6`, `wkS2e`, `wkS3e` — N nested
  `natrec` successor branches leave N stacked substitutions over N weakenings.
  Built by **naturality** (`wk-sub-tm`, one `extS` layer at a time), *not* by the
  collapse recipe `wkS2` uses: fusing the renamings leaves a pointwise goal that
  is **false at `vz`**.
- **`mhAt` / `redAt` / `appAt` / `cong₃g`** — a `subst` motive containing `_`
  under a binder is unsolvable; the same equation as a first-order argument lets
  the use site supply everything.
- **`RecCall` + `recCert`/`recRed`** — "the step recurses at `(A,B)` with some
  certificate", plus projections so the certificate can be fed to `aux-cycle` as
  a **family** in `ih`.

## Standing lessons

- **Agda cannot invert `subTm`.** Anything occurring only under a substitution
  must be **named**, never left as `_`. The signature of this failure is an
  *unsolved meta*, never a wrong solution.
- **Green ≠ meaningful.** `--safe`, zero holes and a green build still prove
  nothing if the premise is unsatisfiable. For every conditional lemma, ask
  whether the premise is dischargeable *at the arguments it is stated for*, and
  write the witness. `check-formers.sh` check 6 is the review list — but note it
  reads a mere **mention in a comment** as a consumer, so a named-but-deleted
  lemma is the honest way to retire one.
