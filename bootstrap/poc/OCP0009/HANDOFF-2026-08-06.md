## 3. WHAT `ordtr` COST, AND THE FOUR THINGS WORTH RE-READING

### (a) ★ The ξ rules MUST be serialized, or `snr-det` is FALSE

Three scrutinees means `ordtr (app (lam s) v) (app (lam s') v') u p q`
head-steps two ways. So each ξ demands that the bounds BEFORE it already
expose a numeral head:

    snr-ordtrᵃ   any a
    snr-ordtrᵗ   under `nsuc a`
    snr-ordtrᵘᶻ  under `nsuc a` + `nzero`
    snr-ordtrᵘˢ  under `nsuc a` + `nsuc t`

`nzero`/`nsuc` have no head step, so every cross pair in `snr-det` is
refuted by an absurd `SNRed`. **The order is `ordstk?`'s own dispatch
order**, and `sn-ordtr`, `homNatSem` and `ordtrS` all walk it — which is
why the five root rules line up with the five numeral leaves one-to-one
in all three.

⚠ `sne-whred` is NOT "neutrals do not step" — `sne-natrec` +
`snr-natrecⁿ` already coexist. The key means the ROOT rule never fires,
not that no subterm steps.

### (b) ★★ Why `fund`'s `⊢ordtr` case has NO conversion plumbing

Level-1 membership IGNORES the reduction chain — every leaf `homNatSem`
lands on (`⊩₁Hom`/`⊩₁Unit`/`⊩₁base`) has `_ ⊩₁∋ x = SN x`. So the whole
semantic obligation is **`SN (ordtr aI tI uI pI qI)`**. No `irrel₁`, no
`relTy`, no motive to re-instantiate. All the content is `sn-ordtr` in
LR, where it belongs: it is a fact about the head strategy, not about
substitution.

`sn-ordtr`'s termination is LEXICOGRAPHIC on the three `NatMem`s:
`a`-steps shrink the first, `t`-steps the second with the first fixed,
`u`-steps the third — and `ordtr-sss`, transitivity's actual content,
shrinks all three at once. **No fuel, no `Acc`, no measure.**

⚠⚠ **TWO PLACES THE "MEMBERSHIP IS JUST SN" SHORTCUT DOES NOT TYPECHECK**,
and both cost a real lemma. The identification is SEMANTICALLY right and
DEFINITIONALLY unavailable — do not assume it on the nose:

* `homNatSem` MATCHES on the two `NatMem`s, so with those opaque it is
  STUCK and never exposes the `SN x` leaf → **`homNatSem-mem`**.
* inside that lemma, `bwd₁ q R ⊩₁∋ x` is stuck whenever `R` is not in
  constructor form → **`bwd₁-mem⁻`**, the level-1 peer of `bwd₀-mem⁻`,
  never needed before. Every row is `h` and it is still load-bearing.

### (c) ★ A SplitError is reported at the WRONG PLACE

FundSN's `snr-anti` failed with `SplitError.UnificationStuck` blaming
`snr-βfst`. The real cause was upstream: `snr-anti` splits on the
