## 2. STEP 6 PROPER — the front, stated exactly

`IHAt` (line 301) is the **statement**; it is not proved.

    IHAt : {Θ : Ctx} (ρ : Ren ⌊ Δ ⌋ ⌊ Θ ⌋) (P : RTm ((⌊ Δ ⌋ ∙) ∙))
         → (k : RTm ⌊ Θ ⌋) → …

**The gap, which is the whole content of step 6:**

- `IHAt` speaks about **`amrec y`**;
- `IndPW` speaks about the **HANDLE's `ihS-atP`**.

They are the same term, and `ihCall-amrec` — the renaming-indexed bridge,
already proved — is exactly that equation. `⊢transportP` moves `P` across it.

⇒ **Step 6 is: instantiate the IH, then transport.**

⚠ **AND THE CERTIFICATE HAS TO BE BUILT.** `IndPW` hands over
`q : nsuc (μ y) ≤ μ a`, while `IHAt` wants `nsuc (μ y) ≤ k`. That
weakening is a separate obligation — do not assume it comes for free.

**Concretely:** instantiate the IH → compose the certificate by `⊢trans` →
transport across `ihCall-amrec` with `⊢transportP`.
