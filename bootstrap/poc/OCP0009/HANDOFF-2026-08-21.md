## 2. ⚠⚠ WHAT IS AND IS NOT ESTABLISHED — do not overclaim

**Established.**

1. `amrecInd` is a total function; green with no escape hatches.
2. **Non-vacuity of the bound.** The `natrec` is instantiated at
   `n := suc (μ x)` and applied to `x` and `⊢le-refl` — so the STRICT
   certificate `μ x < n` is satisfiable at the very carrier being proved
   about. This is *not* `lexrec`'s failure mode
   (`lexrec-branches-done-assembly-open`: an unsatisfiable premise, green
   throughout).
3. **Callability.** `NbEPDirDBSpikeAmrecIndSat.agda` (3.9s) discharges
   `IndStep` at the closed motive `⌜Unit⌝` and CALLS `amrecInd`. If the
   seven-premise interface did not compose, that would not typecheck.

**NOT established.** That check's step **ignores its `IndPW` hypothesis**,
because exercising `IndPW` needs a carrier whose measure genuinely
DECREASES — i.e. a real client. So nothing yet shows the induction
hypothesis is *usable*, only that it is *available*.

⇒ **The success criterion fixed in advance stands and must not drift:**
build the combinator once, then check that **all three** of
`gcd(a,b) ∣ a`, `gcd(a,b) ∣ b`, and maximality go *through* it. Three
customers vindicates the axis; three bespoke rebuilds is the warning sign.

## 4. Lessons worth carrying (added today)

- **`⊢transportP` demands BOTH endpoints typed.** That is why `⊢ihS-atP`
  had to exist: the library types `ihS-atR` (the handle at an ARBITRARY
  renaming) because the irrelevance layer consumes it under binders, and
  nobody had ever needed the un-renamed twin.
- **`IHAt`/`IndBAt`/`IndPW` are DEFINED functions.** Agda unfolds rather
  than decomposing them, so implicit `P`/`ρ`/`k` land under
  `renTm (extR (extR ρ))` and never solve. **Pin them at every call site.**
- **One law generic in σ beats one peel per branch.** `IndBAt-sub` served
  the zero branch (`single nzero`), the successor branch (`nrs`) AND the
  final instantiation (`single N`) — and `IndBAt-ren` fell out of it for
  free, because a renaming IS a substitution (`ren-subTy'`).
- **Extend a module instantiation rather than adding a second one.**
  `Handle` = `AmTΠ` + `⊢ihS-atP`; opening `AmTΠ-at` *and* a second module
  carrying `⊢ihS-atP` would instantiate `AmTΠ` twice at the same parameters.
