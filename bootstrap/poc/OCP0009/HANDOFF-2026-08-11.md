### ⭐ THE THREE FINDINGS, in the order they will matter again

1. **`dmX` was never a miscount.** The previous session's guessed
   derivation `⊢wk³ (⊢wkᶠ³ dm₁)` and its bridge `wᶠ³-single` were both
   right; what was wrong is **which way the `subst` runs**. Probing it
   alone, as this file instructed, took one attempt. ⭐ The four
   derivations that followed went green first try, second try, first try,
   third try — every failure a misplaced cast or one ⊢wk too many.
2. **The `dmX` shape is DEPTH-INDEPENDENT.** (0,S) uses
   `⊢wk³ (⊢wkᶠ⁵ dm)`, (S,S) uses `⊢wk³ (⊢wkᶠ⁵ dm)` — always exactly
   THREE ordinary weakenings (`le`, `lt`, the `y` binder) above the
   carrier slot, and the `⊢wkᶠ` count is whatever is below it. Write it
   that way rather than counting from the top.
3. **Under families the n₂ RESET is not an application.** (S,S)'s rec₁
   instantiates the outer IH's `Π Nat` at `μ₂ y`, which is `w (wᶠ⁸ m₂)` —
   a term. Under codes-and-functions it was `app (w¹⁰ μ₂) (var (vs vz))`,
   a redex that never reduces, and it appeared inside every subsequent
   substitution. That is the single clearest place the interface pays.

  unchanged module reports only load time — measured 2.80 s against 8.73 s
  cold, a 3× understatement. `touch` does NOT invalidate it;

* ⚠ **RSS varies ±12% run to run.** One sample each; a <15% difference
  between modules is not evidence.
* ⚠ **`w` vs `wᶠ`, and `⊢wk` vs `⊢wkᶠ`.** `wᶠ` inserts a slot BELOW a
  family's own variable. They produce terms that look interchangeable and
  are not — but note **the count is wrong more often than the kind**: both
  bugs in amrec's successor branch were off-by-one, not the wrong lemma.
* ⚠ **P1 (in `LibWk`'s header): eta covers every naturality lemma EXCEPT
  moving a family under a renaming.** Those need a pointwise bridge.

  amrec's branches and in lexrec's rec₁ — was a miscounted weakening, never
  a design problem. Put `⊢nzero` where the derivation goes; the
  `UnequalTerms` error prints the goal in full. And probe **one premise at
  a time**.
